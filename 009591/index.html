THE LEGEND OF PoS
Hubert Haider
January 2026
 

The Poverty-of-Stimulus (PoS) argument is central to Universal Grammar (UG). UG is central for Generative grammars. Without UG, no Generative grammars; without PoS no UG. PoS arguments are flawed, UG is dispensable, but grammars are acquired nevertheless, as they have been shaped by cognitive evolution.
