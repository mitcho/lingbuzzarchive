Mixed agreement vs. case assignment patterns
Adam Przepiórkowski
July 2025
 

The paper proposes a novel approach to the mixed agreement vs. case assignment pattern in Slavic numerals and juxtaposes the LFG formalization of this approach with the earlier Minimimalist analysis presented in detail in Concord is Insatiable Agree: Heterogeneous Case and Phi Patterns in Slavic and Finnic.
