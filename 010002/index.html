The logic of the English auxiliary system
Yusuke Kubota, Bob Levine
May 2026
 

This paper proposes an analysis of English auxiliary verbs in Hybrid Type-Logical Grammar. The proposed analysis integrates the key analytic ideas of both transformational and nontransformational approaches in the literature within a logic-based architecture of grammar in which the notion of (formal) inference plays a central role. We show that the proposed analysis not only provides principled and explicit analyses of major properties of English auxiliary verbs but also has interesting implications for crosslinguistic and diachronic aspects of the auxiliary system.
