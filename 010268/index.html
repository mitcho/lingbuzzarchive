Optimal Tone Melodies in Shanghainese: A unified OT account of word-level tone sandhi
Jason Mattausch
August 2026
 

This paper presents a unified Optimality Theoretic analysis of left-dominant word-level tone sandhi in Shanghainese. The proposal consists of a single constraint hierarchy capable of accounting for the disparate sandhi behavior initiated by the language's five lexical tones. By appealing to one basic faithfulness condition and one correspondence constraint that formalizes a familiar rightward shifting pattern, the analysis can account for the major tone melodies induced by each of the five tones—from disyllables to tetrasyllabic expressions—with five additional markedness conditions that regulate only tone melodies in general, including an avoidance of adjacent High tones and an avoidance of melody reversal. One additional contour-faithfulness constraint extends the grammar's coverage to accurately predict the monosyllabic citation forms for each tone from its underlying representation.
