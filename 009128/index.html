Building Meanings, Building Connections: A Festschrift in Honor of Makoto Kanazawa and Christopher Tancredi
Yasutada Sudo, Wataru Uegaki
January 2025
 

We are excited to present this web-based Festschrift dedicated to the remarkable careers of Makoto Kanazawa and Christopher Tancredi to mark their 60th birthdays. The contributions they have made, and continue to make, to the field of formal linguistics are vast and profound, and go well beyond their distinguished scholarly achievements. In particular, the vibrant community they have built and their presence in it have had tremendous significance for its past and current members, especially for those of us who have been fortunate to have them as teachers, supervisors, mentors, and/or colleagues. Among other things, the Semantics Research Group deserves a special mention here. It was established in 1994 under their leadership, and to this day, continues to serve as a unique hub for formal semanticists and syntacticians based in the Tokyo area and beyond, where linguists of different theoretical persuasions engage in open-minded scholarly discussion and exchange original ideas. In addition, it has an important function of providing students rare opportunities to receive hands-on training as aspiring researchers, as well as to peek into the world of professional linguists (mainly via conversations over beer after presentations). We personally feel very lucky to have had these experiences as students, which were truly transformative at both professional and personal levels. Makoto and Chris’s unwavering commitment to academic research and the development of young minds has left a lasting mark on the field. The authors of this volume are among those who have benefited from their guidance and mentorship, and are now in turn fostering the next generations of linguists around the world. As we present this Festschrift, we honour not just their past achievements but also the values they embody—sincerity and passion as researchers and earnestness and generosity as educators. These qualities have been the foundation of their success and the cornerstone of the accepting and encouraging community they have built around them. It is our hope that this volume serves as a fitting tribute to their enduring legacy and an inspiration for all those who follow in their footsteps
