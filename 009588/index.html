Algorithmic Etymology via Skeletal Reconstruction
Dillon Barnaby
December 2025
 

We present a deterministic algorithm for reconstructing Proto-Indo-European etymologies from attested daughter language forms. The Barnaby Protocol operates through systematic skeletal extraction and progressive filtering using established sound laws, morphological patterns, and comparative method principles. When applied to inscriptional evidence across Indo-European branches, the protocol narrows from broad possibility sets to specific PIE root candidates through mechanical application of accepted linguistic laws. This method provides a reproducible framework for etymological analysis. For inquiries about this paper dillonbarnaby@proton.me
