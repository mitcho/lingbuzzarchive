Person and aspect in Taushiro split ergativity
Amy Rose Deal, Zachary O'Hagan
June 2025
 

We analyze the morphosyntax of three types of case/agreement splits in Taushiro (isolate; Peru).
  1. Subjects can be ergative only when the subject is at least as high in person as the object.
  2. Objects can be null only when the subject is nominative, not ergative. (We take this split to reflect when clitic-doubling is possible.)
  3. Subjects can be ergative only when aspect is [PERFECTIVE]
We show that the seeming globality of these patterns is no obstacle for a strictly local theory where both case and agreement reflect Agree. The key tools are dependent case by Agree (Clem and Deal To appear) and case sensitivity/case discrimination, which behaves distinctly for pure agreement vs. for clitic-doubling (Deal 2025).
