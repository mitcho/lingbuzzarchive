Hey, wait a minute! If scalar implicatures are presupposed, why do they fail the “Hey, wait a minute!” test?
Ido Benbaji-Elhadad, Omri Doron
September 2026
 

It has recently been argued that scalar implicatures are presuppositional; e.g. that the inference of the sentence "some students passed the test" that not all students passed the test is a presupposition of the sentence (Bassi et al., 2021). A known challenge to this approach is that scalar implicatures do not intuitively feel like presuppositions, and, more concretely, do not pass a well known test that diagnoses presuppositional content; the "Hey, wait a minute! I didn't know that..." test (Shanon 1976, von Fintel 2004). We offer a new way of thinking about this diagnostic that renders the fact that it does not seem to target implicatures unproblematic for their presuppositional analysis.
