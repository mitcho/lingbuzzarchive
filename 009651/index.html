What Can Mongolian Tell Us about Binding?
Chigchi Bai
January 2026
 

Drawing on Mongolian data, this paper proposes that syntactic binding is based on Agree (phi-feature valuation between a predicate-internal subject and a lower element), and sensitive to verbal inflections. Predicate-external binding occurs when this agreement is followed by Move (subject promotion from inside the predicate to outside it), while predicate-internal binding happens without Move. The paper aligns with the pre-minimalist Standard Binding Theory by defining TP as the binding domain for external binding, but uses the minimal phase boundary for internal binding. It argues the Anaphor Agreement Effect stems from the “immaturity” of an internal subject as the binder before inflectional properties are assigned to the predicate, rather than from a capsule-like structure of anaphors.
