Antipassive constructions: Universals and language description
Martin Haspelmath
November 2025
 

This paper discusses antipassive universals, and as a prerequisite for this, the definition of the term antipassive. I suggest that antipassive constructions are best seen as strategies (and not defined functionally), and are best treated as defined in a way that is parallel to the definition of passive constructions. I emphasize that language-particular description is only indirectly related to comparison: It can profit from it via transparency and inspiration, but comparative grammar is primarily concerned with finding universals and (ultimately) their explanations.
