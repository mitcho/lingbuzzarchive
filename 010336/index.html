The Password
Scott Mcknight
September 2026
 

A reader who encounters the companion papers without this piece will find rigorous argument: convergence across Halliday's linguistics, gerontolinguistic evidence, phenomenological philosophy, developmental psychology. The argument stands on its own. But what precedes it is the encounter that made the argument findable at all — a man in an apartment, three years into living inside cultural difference, reading a novel about the first men who held hafts, and feeling something arrive that the categories he had spent his career building could not hold. The haft was always resting in a hand. The password was always waiting in the ivory. What the years built was the capacity to feel it when it arrived.
