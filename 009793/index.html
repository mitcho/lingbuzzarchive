Rooth-Partee conditionals and the symmetry of disjunction
Yael Sharvit
December 2025
 

This paper argues that ‘or’ is inherently symmetric, based on the intuitive equivalence of [If y is P or Q, then x is] and [If y is Q or P, then x is] – referred to as Rooth-Partee conditionals. As observed in Rooth & Partee (1982), the elided phrase in the consequent clause of these conditionals triggers a reading paraphrased as ‘If x is P, y is P and if x is Q, y is Q’. Accounting for that reading proves to be problematic for asymmetric approaches to ‘or'.
