DEMYSTIFYING ERGATIVITY
Hubert Haider
January 2026
 

Facts fall in place and form a coherent picture once myths have been dispelled. Descriptions of ergativity are fraught with grammatical myths. Strikingly, the puzzlement dissolves into nothingness as soon as one is willing to acknowledge the origin of the mystifications, that is, the confusion of subjects and objects (by functionalist typologists as well as by Generativist syntacticians). [v. 1.4: slightly revised].
