Selector-Head Expressions (SHEs) in English
MAKS PANNER
January 2026
 

English contains a small but systematic class of DP-internal expressions whose categorical status is not well captured by the standard labels «determiner», «adjective», «quantifier», or «noun». Canonical members include «the other» and «the rest», and— crucially for the present theory – their closest structural neighbors in the «indefinite ordinal» family such as «the next», «the last», «the former», «the latter», and «the following». We argue that these expressions instantiate a single, typed configuration: a «Selector-Head Expression (SHE)», i.e., a D-licensed selector constituent of the form «[D the] + [Sel X]» that introduces a discourse-anchored selection variable and therefore requires «definite DP closure». The central constituency claim is that the determiner is not merely an external modifier of a nominal head: in selector readings, «the» is the minimal licenser that closes the selector, so that «[the + X]» forms the smallest closed unit, while numerals and NPs («two», «ten people») are specifications of that alreadyclosed selector constituent
