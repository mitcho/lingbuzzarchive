Itinerant Relative Clauses in Tigrinya
Gioia Cacchioli
October 2025
 

This squib investigates the distribution of restrictive relative clauses within the determiner phrase in the Ethio-semitic language Tigrinya. Contrary to the widely held assumption that restrictive relative clauses occupy a fixed positions in the nominal spine, Tigrinya allows them to appear in several prenominal and one postnominal positions without clear interpretive differences. This unexpected flexibility from an understudied language poses a puzzle for current theories of DP structure and raises questions that call for further investigation.
