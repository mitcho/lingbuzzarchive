On the variable strength of only
Luka Crnic
April 2026
 

A distributed analysis of only divides its contribution between two expressions, both of which quantify over alternatives: a subtraction operator and an exhaustification operator. This dual quantification introduces two points at which a restriction of the domain of alternatives can affect the semantic contribution of only to the sentence. We provide a new argument in support of such double dependence on alternatives, drawing on the well-known variability in the strength of the positive presupposition associated with only – in some cases the prejacent appears to be presupposed, while in others the presupposition is weaker, requiring only that either the prejacent or some other focus alternative be true (e.g., Horn 1969 vs. Horn 1996).

[The paper is a slight revision of the second part of this 2022 paper, the first part of which appeared in the meantime (doi: 10.1007/s10988-024-09420-w).]
