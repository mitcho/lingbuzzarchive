Learning and Generalizing Stress Patterns with a Sequence-to-Sequence Neural Network
Brandon Prickett, Joe Pater
December 2024
 

We present the first application of modern neural networks to the well studied task of learning word stress systems. We tested our adaptation of a sequence-to-sequence network on the Tesar and Smolensky test set of 124 “languages”, showing that it acquires generalizable representations of stress patterns in a very high proportion of runs. We also show that the model, in an agent-based simulation, is biased toward systematic patterns of stress, despite having the expressive power to memorize its training data.
