Dative and Sententional Subjects in Russian: From Inner States to General Statements
Anton Zimmerling, Maria Trubitsina
July 2025
 

This paper discusses two types of non-canonic subjects in Modern Russian - dative subjects of Dative-Predicative-Structures (DPS) and sentential arguments (finite complement clauses and infinitive phrases). We argue that InfPs raise to subject position in Russian only if a number of formal and communicative conditions are satisfied. InfPs must get a topical feature in order to raise and take the subject position. The raising of sentential arguments is blocked if a dative argument of DPS predicatives is present. The absence of an overt dative subject does not shift the predicative meaning - from SLP predication to general statements - automatically.
