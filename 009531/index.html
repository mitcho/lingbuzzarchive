Theme and mood in culturally equivalent proverbs: grammatical mood as a reflection of social attitude
Soroor Karamloo
August 2025
 

This study compares equivalent proverbs in English (eng), Persian (fas), and Spanish (spa) using Halliday’s (1994) SYSTEMIC FUNCTIONAL GRAMMAR (SFG). The motivation is twofold: first, while comparative studies using SFG exist, none address proverbs across these three languages; second, SFG offers an effective framework for analyzing clause structures and linguistic similarities. The hypothesis assumes that equivalent proverbs should be syntactically and pragmatically similar, despite structural and cultural differences. The corpus comprises 774 proverbs—258 from each language—sourced from monolingual dictionaries. Analysis focuses on two METAFUNCTIONS: INTERPERSONAL and TEXTUAL. INTERPERSONAL findings reveal that all three languages favor declarative clauses, indicating an informational orientation and pragmatic equivalence. TEXTUAL analysis shows greater syntactic proximity between English and Persian, particularly in Theme types. Overall, while pragmatic patterns are consistent across the corpora, English and Persian demonstrate stronger SYNTACTIC ALIGNMENT.
