Tropes and Events as Truthmakers
Friederike Moltmann
October 2025
 

This is the appendix of Chapter 2 of my book Abstract Objects and the Semantics of Natural Language, Oxford University Press, Oxford, 2013,p. 89-94. This piece is of particular interest because it discusses the role of events and tropes as potential truthmakers at a time prior to the development of Kit Fine's truthmaker semantics, making use of an older, metaphysical notion of truthmaking (grounding the truth of sentences in things in the world). Chapter 2 itself made use of a Davidsonian view of events and tropes, and thus not as truthmakers.
