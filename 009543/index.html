The Dialectology of Evidential Strategies in Georgian
Thomas Wier
July 2025
 

This paper examines not just how evidentiality functions as a grammatical category in literary Georgian, but also looks at new strategies not found in standard written texts. In many cases, these dialectal constructions show that evidentiality is evolving new forms and functions not typical of the written language: newly grammaticalized suffixes =q'opil and =ot for the unwitnessed past tense, the extension of such forms in some dialects into the nonperfective present and past tenses, and the development of witnessed evidential constructions from earlier confirmative particles. It is argued language contact with other regional languages Megrelian, Chechen, Turkish and Azerbaijani may underlie such trends.
