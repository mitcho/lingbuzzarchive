Øut of Order --The Empty Set and Intervention in Featural Relativised Minimality
Giacomo Presotto, Tommaso Mattiuzzi
July 2026
 

This technical note proposes a partial revision of the formalisation of Relativised Minimality (RM; Rizzi1990,Rizzi2004,Rizzi2018) -- the most well-known account of intervention locality effects. RM, as currently defined, draws on set-theoretic relations: Identity, Inclusion, Intersection, and Disjunction. While all are typically used in presentations of the theory, we argue that Disjunction should be excluded, as it entails a formal contradiction. We propose instead that disjunction cases fall outside the scope of RM-computations, as the relevant structural configurations lack an intervener altogether. This move also avoids certain set-theoretical issues involving the empty set.
