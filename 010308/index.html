The VP in Old Frisian
Eric Hoekstra
September 2026
 

This is part IV of the book: "Explorations in Old Frisian Syntax". It offers observations about the VP in Old Frisian. The book consists of five parts, which have been uploaded separately. The five parts are: I. A Broad Outline of Old Frisian. II. The PP in Old Frisian. III. The NP in Old Frisian. IV. The VP in Old Frisian. V. The AP in Old Frisian.
