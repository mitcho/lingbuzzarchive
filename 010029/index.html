Corrigendum to “Extending Flexible Boolean Semantics for the Language of Mathematics”
Shashank Pathak
May 2026
 

This note corrects a previous claim about the number of readings generated for the sentence "The integers and the reals are countable" in the flexible Boolean semantics framework. It is shown that only two readings are derivable, since a third reading is ruled out by independent constraints on the relevant semantic operators. This correction does not affect the main result that the framework still overgenerates interpretations due to structural ambiguity in coordination.
