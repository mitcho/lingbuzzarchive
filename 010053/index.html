Distributional and Inflectional Properties of Quantifying Words in German: Abstract Agreement Chains Revisited
Dorian Roehrs
June 2026
 

Quantificational elements fall into different groups in German. Besides quantificational determiners, there are adjectival quantifiers and indeclinable quantifiers. I demonstrate that (non-numeral) indeclinable quantifiers cannot be preceded by determiners. Rather than proposing that these types of quantifiers (or parts of them) are in the DP-layer, I argue that these cases can be explained by Bayer's (2015) agreement chain. If so, this means that these quantifiers can appear below the DP-layer.
