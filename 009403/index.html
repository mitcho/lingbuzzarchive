A′-movement restrictions in Igbo reanalyzed
Jasper Jian, Martina Martinović
October 2025
 

This paper provides a novel analysis for A'-extraction bans in Igbo clauses which involve a preverbal harmonizing morpheme E/A. We argue that clauses with E/A which ban extraction involve a CP-layer, which is in competition with the layer required in A'-movement. Our analysis of E/A accounts for its co-occurrence with A'-extraction restrictions, and also predicts when its appearance does not correspond with an A'-extraction restriction. We point to several systematic differences between clauses which do and do not permit extraction including their compatibility with subjunctives and the distribution of clitic and non-clitic subjects. Our analysis brings Igbo in line with a broader observation within the Niger-Congo language family that A'-extraction is restricted out of certain clause types due competition in the left periphery.
