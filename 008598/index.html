Anton Zimmerling
November 2024
 

The reviewed collective monograph ( I. Bornkessel-Schlesewsky, A.L.Malchukov, M.Richards (eds.). Scales and hierarchies: A cross-linguistic perspective. Berlin: Mouton de Gruyter, 2015. 356 p.) discusses the status of feature scales and hierarchies in descriptions and explanatory models of natural languages. The reviewer evaluated the attempts of the authors to validate the existence vs non-existence of feature scales by mathematical methods and methods of natural sciences.
