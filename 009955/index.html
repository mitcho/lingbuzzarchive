Animal semantics [Encyclopedia entry]
Jeremy Kuhn, Mélissa Berthet
May 2026
 

Semantics, the study of meaning, plays a central role in research on animal communication, with the goal of uncovering and understanding homologies or analogies to the way that meaning is communicated in human language. One challenge is that, unlike human language (which can be studied with direct introspective judgments), animal meaning can only be investigated with indirect measures. Recent work has shown that concepts and tools from formal semantics—built for human language—can nevertheless be successfully applied to the study of animal meaning. In this chapter, we use a formal semantic approach to define meaning, semantic denotation and pragmatic inferences in animal communication, lay out their relevance to several key methodologies, and describe principles for investigating the semantics-pragmatics and syntax-semantics interfaces.
