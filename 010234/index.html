Computational generative grammar. The framework
Pauli Brattico
August 2026
 

Computational generative grammar is a branch of modern linguistics stipulating that all theories and hypotheses must be justified by showing that the data follows from the theory. Due to the adverse complexity profile this level of rigor can only be reached with the aid of computational techniques.
