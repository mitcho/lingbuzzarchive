QUD-sensitive projection in a dynamic semantics with alternatives
Lisa Hofmann
April 2026
 

This paper presents a compositional dynamic system (building on Muskens 1996; Stone 1999; Brasoveanu 2006; Farkas and Bruce 2010; AnderBois et al. 2015), in which question alternatives are introduced via the random assignment of propositional discourse referents. This system provides a framework for implementing a mechanism for presupposition projection that is sensitive to the question under discussion (QUD; Ginzburg 1996; Roberts 2012; Büring 2003, Farkas and Bruce 2010). The analysis addresses the triggering problem by treating presuppositions as entailments (Abrusán 2011) and derives projection for not-at-issue entailments, following work linking projection to at-issueness (e.g., Simons 2001; Simons et al. 2017; Tonhauser et al. 2018; Degen and Tonhauser 2025). Projection is derived by computing similarity to local context sets for over QUD-induced partitions of the context set, yielding a compositional account of QUD-sensitive projection.
