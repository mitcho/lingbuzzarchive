Strong and weak epistemic operators in assertions, polar questions, and declarative questions: An experimental study of German
Manfred Krifka
January 2026
 

This article investigates the distribution of the strong subjective epistemic operators "sicherlich", "wohl", "wahrscheinlich", "müsste" and the weak subjective epistemic operators "vielleicht", "möglicherweise", "könnte" in polar questions and in declarative questions in German. The motivation for the study is the hypothesis that in polar questions, the perspective of subjective operators is shifted to the addressee (so-called “interrogative flip”), whereas in declarative questions it remains with the speaker. The article hypothesizes that under this assumption, weak operators should be rated better in polar questions, whereas strong operators should get a higher rating in declarative questions. This is confirmed by an experiment, which consisted in 280 participants in a within-subject rating of assertions, polar questions and declarative sentences with these subjective epistemic operators. The article motivates the guiding hypotheses by pragmatic considerations, and attempts to explain the slightly deviating behavior of the discourse particle wohl.
