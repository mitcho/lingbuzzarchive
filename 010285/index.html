A representationalist theory of the semantics of reciprocals
Shuichi Yatabe
September 2026
 

As has been noted in the literature, a reciprocal pronoun can have two or more separate antecedents simultaneously, as in "Tom shouted and Mary cried each other's names", contradicting all previous accounts of the syntax and semantics of reciprocals. This fact and some related observations are shown to motivate a representationalist theory of the syntax-semantics interface in which the semantic representation of each lexical item is a set of elementary predications and semantic composition is allowed to involve taking the union of such sets.
