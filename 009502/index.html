Argument Ellipsis or More? Adjunct Recoverability in Turkish Object Gaps
Yasemin Fincan
September 2025
 

This paper reexamines Turkish object-gap constructions and tests whether they are derived by Argument Ellipsis or a verb-stranding ellipsis mechanism. The AE-only view predicts that adjuncts can never be interpreted inside the gap. New experimental evidence shows that Turkish speakers allow adjunct-inclusive readings even in the basic antecedent–negated ellipsis configuration that polarity-based accounts label impossible. The results point to a role for verb class. Procedural adjuncts and simple manner adverbs paired with creation verbs allow inclusion, while non-creation verbs behave differently. Neither AE nor polarity ellipsis can explain this pattern, since both predict uniform exclusion. A restricted form of verb-stranding VP ellipsis is needed to capture the data. Turkish therefore expands the typology of head-final languages that require verb-stranding as part of their ellipsis system.
