Parallel Corpora and Their Applications in Translation Studies
Urinboyeva Sevinch
December 2025
 

This article explores the role of parallel corpora in translation studies, focusing on their theoretical significance and practical applications. Parallel corpora, which consist of source texts and their translations, provide valuable empirical data for analyzing translation strategies, equivalence, and cross-linguistic patterns. Drawing on corpus linguistics research and examples from English-based parallel corpora, the article demonstrates how such resources contribute to translation theory, translator training, and applied linguistic research. The study argues that parallel corpora are essential tools for understanding translation as a systematic and data-driven process rather than an intuitive practice.
