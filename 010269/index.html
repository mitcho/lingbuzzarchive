Strict negative concord in Russian and the directionality of Agree
Pavel Rudnev
August 2026
 

This contribution discusses strict negative concord, frequently considered a major argument for Upward Agree whereby feature-defective probes are c-commanded by feature-complete goals. By focusing on the interaction between long-distance scrambling and negative concord in Russian, I formulate a challenge for Upward Agree. The facts are then shown to be trivially compatible with Downward Agree whereby probes c-command goals.
