Null-Argumente in der Deutschen Gebärdensprache (DGS)
Tariq Nazar
December 2025
 

This thesis develops a structural model of argument licensing in German Sign Language (DGS) based on the formal projection of R-Loci. I introduce Locus-Checking as a licensing operation that is informed by generative feature-checking approaches, but adapted to the modality-specific organization of reference in sign languages. While feature-based mechanisms capture important generalizations in spoken languages, the DGS data suggest that argument licensing is more transparently modeled in terms of spatially anchored structures. The resulting analysis situates DGS within Roberts’ (2019) typology and provides the basis for later modal-neutral extensions toward a modal-neutral theory of argument licensing ('R-Anchors').
