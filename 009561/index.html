Null-Argumente in der Deutschen Gebärdensprache (DGS)
Tariq Nazar
December 2025
 

This MA thesis develops a structural model of argument licensing and null arguments in German Sign Language (DGS) based on the formal projection of R-loci. I introduce Locus-Checking as a licensing operation that matches projected argument positions with structurally accessible, referentially specified loci. The analysis shows that patterns of overt and null argument realization in DGS are systematically constrained by the availability and internal structure of R-loci, rather than by verbal agreement morphology or purely discourse-based licensing. On this basis, the thesis offers a structurally grounded perspective on how DGS patterns of argument omission relate to existing typologies of null-argument systems, with particular reference to Roberts (2019).
