(A)symmetries in Kinyarwanda External Possession Constructions
Patrick Kinchsular
April 2023
 

This paper addresses the syntactic properties of two types of external possession constructions (EPCs) in Kinyarwanda. I propose both types involve an external possessor in the Spec of an ApplP. I show that alienable possessors move to the Spec of a HighApplP, while inalienable possessors are base-generated at SpecLowApplP. Asymmetries between the different types of EPCs are attributable to their unique derivations. This paper thus shows that even within a single language, different syntactic configurations can seemingly derive the same semantic interpretation.
