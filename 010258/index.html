The CC1–4 拼 Methodology: From Component Collision to Clausal Packaging (The Four Pins 四拼 Pipeline)
Eddie EddieSLChan
August 2026
 

AXIS2026-M1 (v2) operationalizes the transition from paleographic character components to clausal syntax in Chinese, introducing the Four Pins (四拼) Pipeline: Pinyin (拼音) --> Pinyi (拼義) --> Pinzi (拼字) --> Pinzhi (拼枝). The paper replaces abstract phonological operators with a testable ZTVC (Zero / Tone / Consonant / Vowel) shift analysis and introduces the M7 Clausal Assembler to contrast paratactic Chinese clausal chaining against Germanic hypotaxis. Includes worked case studies on the gǔ (鼓) percussion continuum, the dòu (逗) spatio-temporal pauses, the jū/jù (居) postural shifts, the architectural distinction between zhì (製) and zuò (做), the self-referential bìng/pīn (并 --> 併 --> 拼 --> 摒) cycle, and the cross-linguistic Twin-Idiom Bridge linking jiéjū (拮据) morpheme-by-morpheme to "hand-to-mouth" and "credit crunch." (Note: This unified edition supersedes and retires working papers AXIS2026-M1 v1 and AXIS2026-M2).
