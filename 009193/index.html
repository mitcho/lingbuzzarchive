Clause combining in Tibeto-Burman
Jessica K. Ivani, Balthasar Bickel
July 2025
 

In this contribution, we focus on the clause combining strategies in Tibeto-Burman that have received most prominent attention in the literature: sentential nominalization, converbal clauses, and verb compounding, including serialization. Our main goal is to take stock of what is known about the structural and semantic properties of these strategies and provide a bird's eye view on their variation within Tibeto-Burman. Keeping with scope of the present volume, we refrain from integrating these structures into a universal typology. Instead, we use this review to develop and propose a questionnaire that serves as a basis for such a typology, inspired by previous work (Cristofaro 2003; Bickel 2010; van Gijn et al. 2015; van Gijn 2016). The goal of the questionnaire is to enable future research into the fine-grained variation in Tibeto-Burman clause combining strategies, as these are often characterized by functional and structural overlap.
