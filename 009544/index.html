Language Endangerment in the Caucasus
Thomas Wier
August 2025
 

Though less threatened with extinction than languages of some other parts of the world, the languages of the Caucasus present a number of opportunities to examine language endangerment through a unique sociolinguistic and typological lens. The following paper will survey the state of such endangered languages: how many languages there are, what kinds of language ecologies form their social milieu, and the kinds, rates and causes of language shift in the region. It will also survey the role of language contact in the region, including how processes of multilingualism, borrowing and structural convergence and codeswitching operate in situations of language maintenance, substrate, adstrate and obsolescence effects. It will examine some of the unique structural aspects of endangered languages in the Caucasus, including typological rara and look at how some of these features change: over/under-generalization of marked features, gain/loss of phonological and morphosyntactic contrasts, as well as over-all typological shift.
