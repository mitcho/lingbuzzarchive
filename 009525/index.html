On the Ontology of Truthmakers (handout)
Friederike Moltmann
November 2025
 

This is the handout of a presentation I gave in Kit Fine's truthmaker semantics seminar at NYU on November 18, 2025. Even though the handout is lengthy, it addresses only some of the questions that a central, but underdiscussed issue about truthmaker semantics raises, namely: what sorts of things can be suited for the role of exact truthmaking? The presentation draws various connections to related discussions in metaphysics and to the older notion of truthmaking, namely metaphysical truthmaking. The presentation had to be selective regarding the literature on truthmaking, focusing in part on my own early applications of truthmaking to semantics, which preceded today's truthmaker semantics.
