Some Instances of NP-deletion in French
Richard Kayne, Jean-Yves Pollock
November 2025
 

We have attempted in this paper to shed light on a number of constraints that hold of French NP-deletion. An important role is played by silent elements, one of which, a silent counterpart of French 'quantités', allows an understanding of a surprising asymmetry between plural and mass nouns. We have also been led to consider missing determiners, with the object clitic 'en' playing, against the background of antihomophony, a key role.
