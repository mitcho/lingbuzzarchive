Towards a Propositional Concord Approach for Exclusives in Kasem
Daniel Aremu
February 2025
 

[NB: Just a few updates on the older version] This paper contributes to the ongoing discussion in the literature about the preferred approach for analyzing the syntax-semantic property of the exclusive focus-sensitive particle 'only'. Two approaches have been discussed in the literature due to 'only's syntactic and semantic properties. The first approach is the Quantifier Approach. It involves 'only' composing with its focus associate to form a single quantifier that can quantifier-raise together. The second approach is the Proposition Approach, which involves a high exclusive (null) operator that Agrees with the adfocal realization of 'only' and then licenses the exclusive interpretation. Based on data from an under-studied Mabia (Gur) language, Kasem, and the use of semantic, syntactic, and morphological tests, I argue for a Proposition Approach for Kasem's adfocal-'only' cases. I propose that this can equally account for the adverbial-'only' cases. Overall, the study offers a cross-linguistic support for the Proposition Approach to adfocal-'only' while also raising important questions that morphosyntactic focus marking languages like Kasem present to the analysis of focus-sensitive operators (cf. Lee 2005, Quek & Hirsch 2017, Bayer 2018 2020, Sun 2021, Bassi et al. 2022, Branan & Erlewine 2023, a.o.).
