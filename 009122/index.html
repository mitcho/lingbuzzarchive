Comparing Competing Theories in GrammarXiv: A case study on HPSG and categorial grammar
Yusuke Kubota
July 2025
 

Comparing different theoretical frameworks is often a formidably complex---if not totally impossible---task. The goal of this chapter is to explore the possibilities of GrammarXiv (https://grammarxiv.net; an open-access database of linguistic ‘truths’) for this purpose, taking the comparison of HPSG and categorial grammar (Kubota, 2021) as a test case. The paper illustrates how the graph-based knowledge representation native to GrammarXiv enables an intuitive visualization of highly complex relations between linguistic phenomena, hypotheses and publications within and across competing theories, thereby offering an attractive alternative to the traditional prose-based exposition adopted in Kubota (2021). An additional advantage comes from the open-access, web-based platform of GrammarXiv, which offers a quick and flexible search interface and user-friendly visualization of search results that make domain-specific complex knowledge highly accessible for novices and outsiders.
