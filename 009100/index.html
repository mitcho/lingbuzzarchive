Depend on vs. draw on: Crosslinguistic Insights into Preposition Omission and Semantic Dependencies
Miriam L. Schiele
June 2025
 

English allows omitting Ps in elliptical clauses (e.g., “Peter was talking with someone but I don’t know (with) who”), unlike German (Merchant 2001, Lemke 2021). Nykiel & Hawkins (2020) link the possibility of P-omission to V-P dependency: if a P’s meaning depends on the V, P-omission is preferred. This paper tests that prediction in English and German through two acceptability experiments. Results show a mirror effect: semantic V-P dependencies influence acceptability in both languages, but while English disfavors retaining dependent prepositions, German disfavors omitting them when they are independent.
