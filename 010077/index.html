Laterals patterning with plosives, a piece of evidence from diminutive formation in Modern Frisian
Eric Hoekstra
June 2026
 

This squib presents evidence from Frisian diminutive formation that laterals may pattern with plosives and nasals to the exclusion of fricatives.
