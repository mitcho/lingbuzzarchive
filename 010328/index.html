Referential readings of reflexives
Satoru Ozaki
September 2026
 

I show that reflexives in DP-sized Right Node Raising pivots can have strict readings, just like pronouns in the same environment. Certain properties of these examples preclude an elliptical analysis of Right Node Raising. A multidominant analysis of these examples follows if we assume strict readings come from a referential reading of these reflexives and pronouns. This paper thus supports a treatment of reflexives that distinguish referential from bound readings. Reflexives are like pronouns, except they have to satisfy Condition A.
