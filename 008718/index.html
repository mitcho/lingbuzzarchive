On the learnability of linguistic variation
William Labov
November 2018
 

This note evolved out of many conversations among the authors. It was unfinished and will sadly remain so. It is a programmatic note on how language variation must be learned, in an attempt to provide a mechanistic account of transmission and diffusion discussed in Labov's 2007 Language paper. I (CY) have made no attempt to update the paper with more recent developments or correct residual problems. The core idea seems intact and can be stated simply: the fact of language variation is logically a categorical fact, and must be learned as such first, prior to the tabulation of quantitative and probabilistic information.
