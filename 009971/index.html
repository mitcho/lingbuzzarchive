Ineffability as a window into hidden grammar in Laal
Florian Lionnet
May 2026
 

In this paper, I describe a case of ineffability—the impossibility to produce a morphogically complex form due to specific phonological conditions not being met—in Laal (isolate, Chad). Although the phonological conditionning of ineffability is straightforward and typologically unsurprising, the pattern is nonetheless particularly interesting because of the speakers’ response to the problem of ineffability. Indeed, when asked to derive such forms, speakers, far from simply failing to produce them, make every effort to apply “illicit” phonological operations to produce a form. I argue that the clear choices that speakers make among the many possible such operations they could apply reveals aspects of the phonological grammar of Laal that would otherwise go unnoticed or be misanalyzed.
