Plural and feminine marking in Lunigianese varieties. A(n apparent) case of phonologically-determined affix reordering
Edoardo Cavirani
February 2026
 

Thispaperproposesananalysisofanunexpectedlinearorderingofthegen andnum markers in a set of non-standard Romance varieties spoken in Lunigiana, Italy. This linear ordering is unexpected because it apparently violates the Mirror Principle, which predicts that the gen marker is closer to the root than the num marker. I provide an analysis of the Lunigianese linear ordering that is purely phono- logical, and show that the observed √-num-gen ordering, in fact, does not require a reordering of the corresponding syntactic heads, and can be neatly derived by refining the phonological representation of the relevant markers. This refinement is obtained by employing the formal tools provided by Strict CV and Element Theory.
