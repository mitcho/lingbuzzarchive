Even in phonologically conditioned allomorphy, no escape from syntax
Amazigh Bedar
December 2024
 

This paper explores the nature of the phonology-syntax interface from the perspective of the derivational theories of (morpho)phonology and syntax, with a case study of allomorphy in Taqbaylit Berber. It argues that there is no escaping from syntax even in cases of phonologically-conditioned allomorphy.
