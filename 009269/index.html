Reciprocals and Passives in Ndebele
Asia Pietraszko, Milena Šereikaitė
August 2025
 

This paper examines reciprocal constructions in Zimbabwean Ndebele, marked by the suffix -an. We argue that these reciprocals are verbal, not pronominal, and that -an functions as a valency-reducing verbal extension. Adopting and extending Bruening’s (2004, 2006) analysis, we propose that -an is the exponent of a special type of agentive Voice, (Voice-Rec), which introduces the Agent in its specifier and relates it reciprocally with a lower argument. We further argue that this head bears an obligatory DP licensing feature [∗D∗]ol , giving rise to the requirement that reciprocal constructions must always contain a DP argument. We end with a crosslinguistic connection between Voice-Rec and passives in certain languages.
