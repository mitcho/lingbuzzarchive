Linear A Comparative Linguistic Interpretations and Decipherement of a Minoan Cup Inscription
Anna Eselevich
August 2025
 

This study presents a comparative linguistic analysis of a previously untranslated spiral Linear A inscription on a Middle Minoan IIIa clay drinking cup from Knossos, Crete. Using three distinct methodologies—Proto-Hungarian phonetic mapping, Linear B phonetic equivalence, and Egyptian/Coptic semantic analogues—this paper explores possible phonetic and semantic interpretations of the inscription. Despite differing linguistic frameworks, all three approaches converge on thematic motifs of light, divine descent, spiritual agency, and celestial influence, suggesting that the text served a ritualistic or poetic function within Bronze Age Minoan religious practice. The analysis demonstrates that cross-linguistic comparative approaches, when combined with modern interpretive frameworks, can yield insights into undeciphered scripts and illuminate broader aspects of Minoan culture, including ritual, cosmology, and material symbolism.
