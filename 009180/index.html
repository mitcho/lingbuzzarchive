Topicality and Non-Canonic Arguments in Russian [Топикальность и неканонические подлежащие в русском языке]
Anton Zimmerling
July 2025
 

I discuss the relevance of the topicality feature for the syntax of non-canonic arguments. Russian non-canonic arguments include dative noun phrases, quantifier phrases, sentential arguments, the expletive element eto and zero subjects with the role-and-reference properties of non-referential Agent/Cause. Canonic subjects case-marked with the nominative case can be used both in active and passive clauses and get the communicative status of both theme and rheme. Non-canonic subjects, except for QPs, are almost exclusively used in the active clauses, where they get a fixed communicative status. Most types of Russian non-canonic subjects have the communicative status of theme in their prototypical uses, while Russian QPs typically get the status of rheme. I argue that non-canonic arguments take the subject position according to some subject hierarchy.
