Winnebago Word Prosody and Focused Delimiter Insertion
John Frampton
January 2025
 

Halle and Idsardi (1995}, "General properties of stress and metrical structure," analyzes Winnebago word prosody using the theory of delimiter insertion first developed in Idsardi (1992). In this paper I will show how the idea of focused delimiter insertion allows some substantial improvements in the Halle-Idsardi analysis.
