Morphology, Syntax, and Semantics of Mongolian Causatives: An Interface Analysis
Chigchi Bai
February 2025
 

The main purpose of this paper is to classify and characterize Mongolian causatives according to their morphological, syntactic and semantic properties. Adopting a typological method, this paper demonstrates that to characterize a given verb as a causative, its semantics is the key factor and causative semantics is syntactically reflected; while morphology also reflects causative semantics, it is not a crucial factor for characterizing causatives in Mongolian.
