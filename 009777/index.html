What Does 怀疑 (huaiyi) Mean? An Investigation into the Semantics of the Dubitative Verb in Mandarin Chinese
Yunchong Huang
August 2020
 

This thesis attempts to investigate the semantics of the dubitative verb 怀疑(huaiyi) in Mandarin Chinese. Dubitative verbs have always been an interesting topic in the field of syntax and semantics cross-linguistically because of their debatable denotations and peculiar complementation patterns. 怀疑(huaiyi) is yet more complicated due to its ambiguity between two empirical interpretations—the first is "having a tendency not to believe" (huaiyi1), and the second one is "having a tendency to believe" (huaiyi2). Therefore, there is an additional disambiguation mechanism at work in the process of its interpretation. This thesis claims that this disambiguation mechanism is based on the information structure and is essentially related to the logical relationship between the content proposition and the immediate contextual evidence as claimed in Lu (2016), mainly depending on the location of the informational focus and the "givenness" status of the content proposition in the embedding complement. As for the denotation of this verb, I claim that it is similar to the hybrid semantics of doubt in English proposed by Anand and Hacquard (2013), but with a crucial difference: the preference assertion is bidirectional and needs to be determined by the aforementioned disambiguation mechanism.
