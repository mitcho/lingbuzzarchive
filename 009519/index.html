The Semantics of Replacement
Friederike Moltmann
November 2025
 

This paper introduces various semantic and syntactic generalizations about replacement verbs and instead-phrases in English and outlines a unified semantics based on the notion of a structured whole and the notion of a slot within a structured whole.
