A Dialectometric Analysis of Lexical Variation across regions of Kazakhstan
Alinur Abildayev
September 2025
 

This paper studies how lexical variation differs across the main dialect zones of the Kazakh language. I use survey data from 40 native speakers in Southern, Western, and Northeastern regions of Kazakhstan, where participants provided word choices for everyday objects. Our results show that the Southern dialect displays the widest variation and frequent Russian borrowings, the Northeastern dialect is the most consistent with limited variation, and the Western dialect stands out as the most distinct with unique lexical items. I also find that 80% of participants report experiencing misunderstandings due to regional vocabulary differences
