Universal Noun Theory: A Linguistic Exploration of Nominal Adaptability
Jayant Kaushik
March 2025
 

This paper proposes the Universal Noun Theory (UNT), which states that any word in a language can function as a noun when it is used as a name. This challenges traditional grammatical categories and highlights the flexibility of language. UNT builds on existing linguistic theories such as functional shift, Saussurean arbitrariness, and Wittgenstein's language games, yet presents a novel claim: the noun is the most adaptable category, capable of absorbing all words when contextually assigned as a proper noun. Through theoretical analysis and practical experimentation, this paper examines the implications of UNT for syntax, semantics, computational linguistics, and cognitive processing.
