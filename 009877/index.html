Integrated and Abstract Wholes: Prospects for a Constructional Ontology as Part of Universal Grammar (handout)
Friederike Moltmann
March 2026
 

This talk discusses the view of constructional ontology and mereological pluralism in connection with generative grammar and argues for a dual part structure of artefacts reflected in two nouns 'part' in English and at least other European languages.
