The shapes of meaning: Diagrams in lexical semantics
Joost Zwarts
September 2026
 

Diagrams in lexical semantics come in all shapes and sizes, but there is currently no good understanding of the design space that allows for this variety. This paper derives that space from the combination of a small number of graphic and semantic parameters, thereby contributing to our insight in this increasingly common visual metalanguage in linguistics and related disciplines concerned with meaning.
