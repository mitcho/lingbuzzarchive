Nandao-Qs: When surprise sparks inquiry
Anshun Zheng
June 2025
 

This paper investigates the semantics and pragmatics of nandao questions (nandao-Qs) in Mandarin, focusing on their evidential properties. While previous studies have treated nandao as a biased question word or a rhetorical device (Alleton 1988, Gong 1995, Xu 2012, 2017), this paper argues that nandao-Qs encode evidential signals and reflect the speaker's confrontation with unexpected contextual evidence. Drawing on insights from the Kernel-based framework from Von Fintel & Gillies (2010), this paper demonstrates that nandao-Qs marks an incompatible Kernel, representing an "epistemic" conflict that arises from either disbelief or a deviation from grammatical priors. Additionally, I explore the selectional constraints of nandao, explaining its exclusive compatibility with polar questions. Furthermore, I revisit the relationship between nandao-Qs and rhetorical questions, establishing that while nandao creates an environment conducive to rhetorical interpretation, it does not inherently constitute a rhetorical question. This analysis offers a unified account that integrates previous approaches and sheds light on giving explanations for the broader discourse functions of nandao.
