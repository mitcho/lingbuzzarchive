Code-Switching Sentence Processing in Bilingual Adults
Azka Imtiaz
November 2025
 

This study explores how code-switching affects sentence comprehension and reading speed in bilingual adults. Forty participants, fluent in English and Urdu, completed a self-paced reading task with sentences containing code-switched elements and fully monolingual sentences. Results showed that reading code-switched sentences took longer, while comprehension accuracy remained largely unchanged. These findings suggest that bilinguals can manage dual-language activation effectively, maintaining understanding even when cognitive load increases. The study provides insights into bilingual sentence processing and has implications for both theoretical linguistics and applied language studies.
