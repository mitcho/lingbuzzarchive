The Role of the Possessor/HAVE-Relation in Semantics (handout)
Friederike Moltmann
March 2026
 

It is a common view that the wide range of readings of the possessor construction and of 'have' are due to polysemy. The talk presents semantic data that require a unified have-relation even in the absence of HAVE in syntax. It will pursue a unified relation along the lines of a 'reference-point relation' in the sense of Langacker (1993).
