Machine yearning: LLMs do not capture formal linguistic structure and obscure neuroscientific inquiry
Elliot Murphy, Paolo Morosi, Evelina Leivada, Andrew Ira Nevins
April 2026
 

Futrell and Mahowald (2025) argue that neural networks have learned non-trivial aspects of language. We argue that these systems have not in fact demonstrated “mastery” (2025: 10) of syntax, marshalling recent evidence, and that they further serve to obscure explanatory insights with respect to topics in the cognitive neuroscience of language.
