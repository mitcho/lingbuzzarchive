Anaphoric Definiteness in Korean Sign Language: The role of NPLOC and NP-IXTHIS
Arum Kang, Youngju Choi, Min-Joo Kim
September 2025
 

This paper investigates how Korean Sign Language marks anaphoric definiteness, focusing on NPLOC and NP-IXTHIS. Two central claims are that in Korean Sign Language, NPLOC is a strong definite comparable to demonstrative anaphoric NPs in spoken Korean, and that NP-IXTHIS, which combines a nominal with simultaneous pointing, encodes anaphoric noteworthiness, paralleling emphatic demonstratives like “this very N” in spoken English. We propose a formal semantic analysis of NPLOC and NP-IXTHIS, adopting Kim’s (2023b) locative-relation based framework, in which indexing and locus are separated from each other. The proposed analysis will be shown to have implications for anaphoric definiteness marking in American Sign Language as well as other (bare noun) languages.
