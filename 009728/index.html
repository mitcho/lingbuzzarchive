The Morphodynamics of Y as a Ghost Connector Relational Phonology and Directed Vowel Trajectories
MAKS PANNER
February 2026
 

This article argues that Y is neither a vowel, nor a glide, nor an energy conduit. Y is instead a ghost connector: a silent predicator and directional operator that assigns transitions between vowel states within a universal vowel well. Under this reconceptualization, English “diphthongs” are not vowel blends and not vowel+glide sequences. Their nucleus is a single trajectory object—a predicated transition—generated by an operator of the form Y(v₁ , v₂ ) and not representable as a static feature bundle. In CVC structures, Y never fills the V-slot; the trajectory fills it. The account also rejects an empty-nucleus analysis and does not treat Y as a governing/licensing head; instead, nucleus formation is typed as state versus trajectory. Finally, the theory connects abstract predication to physical realization through a commutative interface diagram, preserving systematic correspondence while rejecting identity between morphodynamical transitions and articulatory gestures.
