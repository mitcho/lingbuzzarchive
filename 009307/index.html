Neurological Representations of Linguistic Intuitions and World Knowledge Intuitions: a Case Study of Evdientiality
Yurie Hara
September 2025
 

This chapter aims to dissociate linguistic intuitions and world knowledge intuitions by re-examining the introspection-based judgments of Japanese modality and evidentiality reported in Hara & Davis (2013); Davis & Hara (2014) and Hara (2017) as well as the results of an electroencephalography (EEG) experiment conducted by Hara, Orita, Ying, Koshizuka & Sakai (2020).
