Metaphony in Substance Free Logical Phonology
Kyle Gorman, Charles Reiss
August 2024
 

We provide an overview of Substance Free Logical Phonology (LP) and apply this model to a few case studies of synchronic metaphony and similar processes. We adopt Inkelas's idea of exceptionality as prespecification, along with the implementation of feature filling rules based on a partial operation called unification to show that some cases of metaphony that are treated as morphophonological and/or morphosyntactic can instead be handled by LP in narrow phonology. We also explain how Inkelas's suggestion for rule targets extends to rule triggers, as well.
