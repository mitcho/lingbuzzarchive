Thinking Inside the Box: Genitive Subject Licensing in Uyghur under the Miracle Creed System
tao xie
June 2026
 

This paper revisits the licensing of genitive subjects in Uyghur embedded clauses. Building on Asarina and Hartman’s (2011) D-licensing analysis, we argue that the simplified Miracle Creed system provides a more principled account of the relevant facts. We first show that the D-licensing approach is largely compatible with the MC architecture. Adopting the view that boxed elements created by Internal Merge are accessible to higher phase heads, we propose that phase-head access can be reduced to a target-conditioned application of Minimal Search. Under this approach, the licensing of genitive subjects, the optional omission of genitive morphology, scope asymmetries between genitive and unmarked subjects, and the contrast between modal and non-modal predicates receive a unified explanation. Furthermore, locality conditions such as the Phase Impenetrability Condition need not be stipulated independently. The Uyghur data thus provide empirical support for an interface-oriented interpretation of the MC system.
