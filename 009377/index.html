Logic and innateness. What is the connection between them?
Viktor Dudkevych, Olha Kovalchuk
April 2025
 

The article is designed to attract the attention of those who deeply study the meanings of words that are effective in practice and do not mislead readers. The authors of this article call for refining the meaning of the word “logic” and clarifying its meaning to see the relationship of logic to innateness. The same article also describes the meaning of “logical method” as well as “ethics” as the natural opposite of logic. It will also be important in this article to explain that there are only two kinds of logic in nature: abstract logic and concrete logic. Therefore, this article will be useful for the development of science and a better explanation of the meaning of words and tools of thinking.
