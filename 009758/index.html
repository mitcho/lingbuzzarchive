The Tense-Mood-Aspect System of Makista
Hao Lin
January 2022
 

Makista, the Macao Portuguese Creole, is an understudied endangered language. This essay explores the Tense-Mood-Aspect(TMA) system of Makista by considering the grammatical and semantic properties of the three overt TMA markers: já, lôgo and ta. Examples are collected from José dos Santos Ferreiro's poems and existing scholarly literature by Pinharanda-Nunes. Results reveal that já is a past marker in tense, and a perfective marker in aspect; lôgo is a future marker in tense, and an irrealis marker in mood; ta is a non-punctual marker in aspect. The study provides empirical evidence for the research of Ibero-Romancebased creoles, and contributes to the field of creole study.
