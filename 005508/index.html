The VO-OV split of Germanic languages – a T3 & V2 production
Hubert Haider
October 2020
 

Germanic is the only Indo-European language family that has split into an OV group (continental West Germanic) and a VO-group (English, North-Germanic). Moreover, Germanic is the only Indo-European language family that has developed and maintained the V2 property. The predecessor languages of the modern Indo-European languages are neither OV nor VO, but languages with flexible positioning of V in the VP. OV and VO are the immediate results and alternative outcomes of the fixing of the unspecified directionality of licensing its dependents by the verbal head. V2 was the catalyst for this change.
