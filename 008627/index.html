Copying Variables
Yoshihisa Kitagawa
June 2003
 

This is an editorially corrected version of a previously published article. In this work, it is argued that all types of strict identity interpretations involving ellipsis and anaphora are derived by the covert copying of bound proforms in the course of the derivation toward the C-I interface. It then is pointed out, crucially, that such bound proforms may include bound variables. This approach correctly captures quite pervasive parallelism between some ellipsis constructions and E-type anaphora. The proposed approach can also provide straightforward accounts for certain puzzles concerning agreement and scope of quantified antecedents.
