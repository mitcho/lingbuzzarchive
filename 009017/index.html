The Variable-Force Root Modal in Laz is Gradable
Ömer Demirok, Muhammet Bal
February 2025
 

This study documents the syntactic and semantic properties of the modal suffix -oni in Laz, which contributes a root modal that exhibits force variability. The verbal suffix -oni constructs nonfinite predicates that serve both attributive and predicative functions in the language, akin to infinitivals in English. We argue that it denotes a gradable modal, as evidenced by its ability to participate in comparative structures. The degree-based account in Grano (2024) we adopt for -oni not only explains its force variability but also accounts for how different syntactic contexts restrict it, in line with the Determiner-Modal Generalization (Bhatt, 1999; Hackl & Nissenbaum, 2012).
