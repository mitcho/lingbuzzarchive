A Cartesian dream: A geometrical account of syntax (introduction)
Matteo Greco, Mocci Davide
November 2024
 

The essays in this collection celebrate Andrea Moro's work, which can be viewed as an implementation of what he uses to call a "Cartesian dream": the idea that linguistic phenomena can all be captured in geometrical terms, i.e., by referring to the syntactic configurations generated by assembling lexical items. The distinguished contributors of this volume report their latest research in linguistics – including syntax, computational linguistics, semantics, pragmatics, and neurolinguistics.
