The Morphosyntax of Hasawi Arabic's Tense, Aspect, and Aktionsart
Abdullah Almuhaysh
August 2026
 

This is a paper that passed the MPhil to PhD viva upgrade process. It is part of my work in progress PhD thesis. It deals with the morphosyntax of tense, aspect, and aktionsart of my dialect, Hasawi Arabic. I argue that by scrutinizing the morphosyntax of auxiliary verbs with lexical verbs, one could arrive at the morphosyntactic structure (1), where tense is higher than aspect, and aspect is higher than aktionsart. I show how this is reflected in Hasawi Arabic's morphosyntax by templatic morphology. Being a viva upgrade paper and a work in progress, this paper suffers from several drawbacks, one of which is a lack of literature review on the morphosyntax of Arabic verbs. Comments and scathing criticism are welcome.
