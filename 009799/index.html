ZË-RO v0.1: A Deterministic Orthography Aperture Meter with Baseline-Locked Drift Detection
Sokol Gora
February 2026
 

We present ZË-RO v0.1, a deterministic research instrument that extracts orthographic vowel-carrier structure (A, E, I, O, U, Y, Ë) and computes an aperture proxy using a fixed calibration scale. The system is designed for reproducibility and drift detection: research harnesses emit MD/JSON reports and commit baseline-locked references that fail on unexpected change. We report baseline behavior across multiple suites: positive-signal language datasets (Albanian and Turkish), a strict negative control using pseudowords (no semantic intent), and a drift-visibility suite (Taiwan Zhuyin) designed to weaken under expansion. The negative control shows weak/non-significant slope and no strengthening from STEP10→STEP20, supporting that observed monotonic descent in language datasets is not trivially forced by the harness. This submission reports measurement and reproducibility characteristics of the instrument; it does not claim semantic “proof” by default.
