Romanian Neuter Plural: Both Conservative and Innovative
Jonathan Leibu
September 2025
 

Romanian stands apart from its Western Romance counterparts in the evolution of the Latin neuter. While most Romance languages resolved neuter nouns into either masculine or feminine classes—often reanalyzing neuter plurals (‑a) as feminine singulars—Romanian preserved the neuter as a distinct morphological and syntactic class in the singular, but innovated by assigning feminine plural agreement to neuter nouns. This development constitutes a mirror-image reanalysis: instead of eliminating the neuter distinction through full assimilation, Romanian maintains the category by reinterpreting its plural behavior. The result is a system that is both morphologically conservative and syntactically adaptive, retaining a Latin inheritance in uniquely Romanian terms.
