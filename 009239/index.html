Interaction of Animacy and Number: The Case of Odia, a Classifier Language
Sayantani Banerjee, Chandni Dutta, Biswanath Dash
January 2025
 

This study examines the interaction of animacy and number in Odia, an Eastern Indo-Aryan classifier language with nominative–accusative alignment. We show that animacy in Odia is a formal syntactic feature hosted in a dedicated Anim head above DP. [+Animate] plurals are marked with maan and optionally -e, while [-Animate] plurals take guDika. Only animate plurals trigger verbal number agreement; inanimate plurals receive default singular agreement unless discourse context induces an animate interpretation. Applying Wiltschko’s (2009) diagnostics, we demonstrate that Odia animacy is context-sensitive, formally encoded, and capable of driving agreement operations. Structurally, -e attaches to maan at n, and the resulting AnimP triggers clausal agreement. This work highlights how formal animacy can shape agreement and classifier use in a classifier language.
