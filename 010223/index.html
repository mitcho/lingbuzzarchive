2nd Deng of Middle Chinese rhymes—A unique phonation genre preserved among Chinese dialects
Yanguang炎光 Guo郭
August 2026
 

Previously the author has deciphered that 2nd Deng from Yunjing designates a glottal medial in relevant syllables of Middle Chinese rhyme books. Generally, there is little chance for word-internal glottle stops to be avilable to form CʔV syllables. The author observed there are unique waveforms of vocal fry(even series of single pulse fry, with damped pulse characteristics) phonation among vowels of words of 2nd Deng . Infered from this observation, by exploring words of 2nd Deng under non-Zhuang initials, large volumes of sounds containing phonation of vocal fry are identified among sounds from branches of Chinese dialects of Yue, Min, Hakka and Wu. [ɐ̰] and other vowels with vocal fry phonation had been functioning among Middle Chinese words under 2nd Deng, constrastive to the same catergory of rhymes with modal voice among words under 1st Deng. Aided with research results over glottal stops and creaky voice phonation it is concluded that all those words of non-Zhuang initials under 2nd Deng form CʔV syllables and contain glottal medials which are solely phonetic perpception cued by low F0 of vowels with vocal fry phonation. Key word: 2nd Deng Glottal medial Vocal fry(even series of single pulse fry, with damped pulse characteristics) 作者之前破解了《韵镜》里二等的语音性质是以喉塞清音[ʔ]作为介音。一般认为,缘于汉语声韵调的特点,在常见主干音节CV结构中在元音之前,存在喉塞清音的可能性很低。作者观察到吴方言下属音系中个别中古音二等例字其元音的声波波形为气泡音,以此为线索,以中古音非莊组声纽下麻韵江耕韵佳韵庚等等二等韵系字作为参考,成功地在闽方言粤方言客家话吴方言等下属诸多音系的二等例字发音中找到了气泡音(均匀单脉冲连续波气泡音) 发声态。作为一类较为特殊的发声态,气泡音应曾广泛地存在于中古音汉语音系中,非莊组二等韵系的主元音应以气泡音作为发声态,其与中古音中发声态为常态的一等韵系两两形成对立,由此解决了中古音中一等韵系二等韵系的拟音难题。基于中古音韵图中韵系的排列以及跟随莊组声纽的韵系也为二等,并借鉴国际语音学界对嘎裂声和喉塞音的研究成果,可以推导出中古音二等须处理为喉塞介音。在非莊组声纽下二等韵系例字中,该类喉塞介音在音节中的位置处于声母和发声态为气泡音的元音之间,此类喉塞介音主要是通过对特征声学指标提示的感知方式来实现。 关键词:《韵镜》二等 喉塞介音 气泡音发声态
