Children’s acquisition of the meaning of disjunction
Alexandre Cremers, Lyn Tieu
August 2026
 

The question of how young children interpret disjunctive words like English “or” has become a topic of increased attention in the recent developmental semantic/pragmatic literature. We present a thematic overview of the past half century of research on children’s acquisition of the meaning of disjunction, from the Piagetian era in the 1940s to the most recent experimental pragmatics studies of disjunction. Along the way, we highlight ways in which children differ from adults in their interpretations of disjunction, as well as ongoing debates about the sources of these interpretations, with the hope that a historical perspective may shed light on these recent debates.
