Grammatical Tone in Igbo Verbs
Ferdinand Duru, Jack Pruett
August 2025
 

This paper analyzes the understudied grammatical tone system in Igbo verbal paradigms, highlighting a distinction between two types of TAM morphemes: (i) those without floating tones, which do not override the verb root’s underlying tone, and (ii) those with floating tones, which do. The results of our novel analysis demonstrate that floating high grammatical tones trigger epenthesis of an extra Tone Bearing Unit (a mora), while floating low grammatical tones override the verb root’s underlying tone. The study offers a novel perspective on Igbo tone and suggests directions for future research.
