Optimising Daylight for Human Wellbeing: in Appenzell Innerhoden & Ausserhoden
Al Dhali
October 2025
 

Daylight plays a central role in human health and wellbeing. Beyond allowing us to see, it reg- ulates our circadian rhythm, mood, alertness, and cognitive function. In mountainous regions like Appenzell (Innerhoden and Ausserhoden), traditional tall houses and complex topographies present unique daylight challenges. This project proposes a novel Fuzzy-AI daylight simulation framework to evaluate and optimise daylight penetration into residential spaces under variable seasonal and weather conditions. The study aims to provide window design guidelines that maximise daylight exposure, preserve architectural heritage, and promote occupant wellbeing.
