Mandarin Third Tone Sandhi in 'Unstructured' Strings: A Stochastic Match Theory Analysis
Jason Mattausch
July 2026
 

This article gives an analysis of Mandarin Chinese third tone sandhi. We focus on the foundations of third tone sandhi by concentrating on strings of digits and lists, commonly referred to as 'unstructured' strings, whose sandhi patterns are not influenced by morphosyntactic structure. We introduce data from a series of elicitation experiments that show, contrary to popular belief, that such strings in fact manifest internal structure and vary radically in terms of the sandhi patterns they exhibit. We analyze a variety of these patterns using a combination of Match Theory and Stochastic Optimality Theory. The former enables the description of the interplay between sandhi domains, metrical feet, and the aforementioned structural properties of the strings, while the latter is a probabilistic model well-suited for modeling interactions among competing factors of different strengths and describing the asymmetric patterns of variation like those found in our empirical data.
