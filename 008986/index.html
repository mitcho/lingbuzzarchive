Alphabeta Meminerunt Syllabaria
Dmitry Okolnikov
February 2025
 

BD FГ MΛ ПТ (𐌚S) ______________ To understand it you may need to see consonants as labials and linguals (this term is not commonly used in linguistics, but they're letters made by the tongue the way labials are made by lips) and then you may notice that liabials tend to look like doubled glyph of the nearby lingual (as lips are a pair and tongue is alone) _________________________________________________ The middle part of it (the twenty letter alphabet) is out of place and I didn't have to include it not to overwhelm the readers. But I did, and it hangs there without an explanation, so let's elaborate on it here: the first line is voiced plosives, the second line is voiced fricatives, the third line is sonors, the fourth line is voiceless plosives, the fifth line is voiceless fricatives. The rudiment of such tendency still can be seen in russian alphabet, which groups voiced consonants in the first half of it, but the latin alphabet shows some somewhat yin-yangish alternation of voiced claster of consonants with a voiceless in the centre of it; voiceless line with a voiced letter in the centre of it; in the next line k is excentric, because the frame shifted; in the other two lines exceptions are placed in the centres of the corresponding groups of consonants. If it was placed there by some wizards of the past or if that is nothing but some fascinating coincidences, it is up to the readers to make up their own minds._____________________But then that additional material demonstrates that the rudiments of this hypothetic protoalphabet syllabary can be seen in alphabets to this day, if the division by lines is applied to the alphabet and knowledge of only two types of consonants (B T in greek myth, M S in jewish myth)
