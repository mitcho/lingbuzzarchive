Iterated Rationality Models and conjunctive readings of disjucntions
Alma Frischoff
August 2024
 

This work proposes a modification of Iterated Rationality Models (IRMs; Franke 2011, Frank and Goodman 2012 a.o.) that replaces the common naive-speaker assumption with a new perspective of the speaker, based on weighted probabilities. This modification resolves a major issue for such models in deriving conjunctive readings for disjunctions (e.g., free choice inferences) with more than two disjuncts, as observed by Franke (2009, 2011), van Rooij (2010) and Fox and Katzir (2021). Unlike previous IRMs, an IRM involving the proposed perspective of the speaker derives such readings, regardless of the number of disjuncts.
