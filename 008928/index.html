Review of Lyle Campbell: Linguist on the loose
Norbert Francis
April 2023
 

In a commentary on this interesting book by L. Campbell (included as a Forward) the author (W. Davis) makes the claim that UG-oriented research on language is in effect an impediment to the endangered languages research program and to the practical efforts on the behalf of language communities. But if hypotheses based on the assumption that knowledge of language is based exclusively on domain-general learning mechanisms can be shown to be incorrect (Chomsky, 1959), then the above claim, besides from being misleading as it reads, becomes irrelevant. Reference: Chomsky, N. (1959). A Review of B. F. Skinner's Verbal Behavior. Language, 35, 1, 26-58.
