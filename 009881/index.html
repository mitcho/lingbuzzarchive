Optionality and variation in Camuno interrogatives: a syntactic analysis
Simona Gabriela Neagu
March 2026
 

This paper examines optionality and variation in interrogative constructions in Camuno, an Eastern Lombard variety. Based on original data from different sub-varieties spoken in Valle Camonica, I show that Camuno displays three main interrogative strategies: wh-fronting, clause-internal wh-elements, and wh-doubling. I argue that these patterns can be accounted for within a unified syntactic analysis in which wh-elements form a single A-bar chain composed of an operator and a restriction. Adopting a partial copying approach, I propose that the fronted element realizes the operator in the CP domain, while the clause-internal element corresponds to the restriction stranded at the vP edge through successive-cyclic movement. Apparent optionality is derived from PF variation in the pronunciation of chain copies, rather than from true free variation. Finally, I suggest that some residual variation is influenced by sociolinguistic factors, in particular contact with standard Italian. The analysis provides further evidence for successive-cyclic A-bar movement and the role of phasal boundaries in interrogative syntax.
