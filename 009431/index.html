Monograph : 'Speaking Brains' (A Summary)
Joseph Galasso
October 2025
 

The arguments herein are that a hybrid AI model is needed to replicate human thought, combining local-domain connectionist models (neural nets) with distant-domain symbolic models (rule-based reasoning). His recent research bridges theoretical linguistics and neuroscience to investigate how the brain processes language, with a particular focus on the roles of the basal ganglia and astrocytes. He compares these neural mechanisms to artificial intelligence (AI) models, arguing that biology provides a more complete model for understanding recursive language functions.
