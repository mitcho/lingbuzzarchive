On the relationship between privative and equipollent feature specifications
Kyle Gorman
May 2026
 

Equipollent and privative feature specifications are formally compared. A bijection is proven between consistent and complete equipollent and privative segments, grounding the intuition that under- and contradictory specifications have no equivalent in privative systems. However, it is shown that there exists no isomorphism between equipollent and privative natural classes, grounding the intuition that the former defines natural classes inexpressible in the latter.
