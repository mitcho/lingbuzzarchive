Itelmen ejectives
Xiaotian Wang, Jonathan David Bobaljik
July 2025
 

This paper offers a preliminary study of ejective consonants in Itelmen (ISO 639-3 itl, previously also known as Kamchadal), a Chukotko-Kamchatkan language traditionally spoken on the Kamchatka peninsula in what is now the Russian Far East. Itelmen is the only Chukotko-Kamchatkan language–and the only language in the area– with an ejective consonant series. We have approached our description with an eye towards presenting sufficient information to situate Itelmen ejectives within a phonological and phonetic typology of ejectives.
