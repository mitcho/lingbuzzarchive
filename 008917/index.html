Interstratal differences in the tonology of Zacatapec Chatino: An argument for Harmonic Layer Theory
Eva Zimmermann
March 2025
 

The tonal phonology of Zacatepec Chatino is highly interesting from a theoretical and typological perspective. Most notably, tone-less tone-bearing units show an asymmetric behaviour across morpho-syntactic domains and are blocked from undergoing regular tone-epenthesis in certain phrasal contexts. I argue that this challenging pattern is best analysed within a cyclic model where tone epenthesis only applies within stems but becomes impossible at the phrase layer. This account is modeled within Harmonic Layer Theory, a novel framework for the morpho-phonology interface where interstratal differences like the epenthesis asymmetry fall out from an activity decay of phonological elements. This model can easily incorporate explanations for a variety of other tonal facts of Zacatepec Chatino, an example being the contrast between floating tones that associate early at the word layer and those that have to wait until the phrase layer — something which is inherently difficult to predict in alternatives like stratal OT that are based on co-grammars.
