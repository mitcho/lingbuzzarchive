Fünetik Alfübet: "Keep It Simple" Phonetic Alphabet
Wesley Wright
April 2026
 

The Fünetik Alfübet is a simplified phonetic writing system designed to make English pronunciation more accessible to beginning and intermediate learners. The system follows a One Sound, One Character principle, employing primarily Latin letters with a small set of intuitive diacritics. It removes schwa as an independent phoneme, instead representing the underlying vowel, and introduces straightforward representations of rhotic vowels using ʊr or vowel+r. Diphthongs are not treated as phonemic sequences but as natural transitions, allowing the system to maintain a primarily monophthongal inventory. The draft presented here (Version 0.9) is based on Canadian English (Southwestern/Southern Ontario) and includes a full reference chart, phonological rationale, dialect notes, and a sample transcription of “The North Wind and the Sun.” The project is released under a CC BY 4.0 license and is open for community feedback from phonologists, phoneticians, ESL practitioners, and others interested in English phonology and pedagogy.
