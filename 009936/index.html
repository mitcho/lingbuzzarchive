Sisterhood as a licensing configuration
Zeljko Boskovic
August 2026
 

The paper argues that Sisterhood needs to be admitted as a feature-licensing configuration, which is implemented through prominent feature sharing involved in labeling. The account proposed in the paper explains and unifies a number of phenomena, like indeterminate licensing, wh-movement in languages like English, the EPP, clitic-strong pronoun (and more generally strong/weak element) alternations, ordering of agreement effects, and certain Case effects. Traditional Spec-Head agreement is quite generally recast as licensing under Sisterhood (though not all relevant Sisterhood licensing involves traditional Spec-Head agreement). It is also shown that Sisterhood licensing and Agree licensing can occur in either order when the same head is involved in both.
