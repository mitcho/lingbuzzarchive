Morphosyntax of A'ingae clauses and verb phrases: A description
Maksymilian Dąbkowski
March 2026
 

This paper describes the morphology and morphosyntax of verbs and clauses in A'ingae (or Cofán, ISO 639-3: con, an Amazonian isolate). A'ingae is characterized by rich, predominantly head-marking morphology, robust pro-drop, relatively free (often verb-final) word order, and nominative-accusative morphosyntactic alignment. Themes emphasized in the description include the functions of A'ingae suffixes and clitics, their morphosyntactic restrictions, and the types of clauses they can appear in.
