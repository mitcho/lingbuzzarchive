A Universal Noun Slot in the Linguistic Mind: Reverse Entailment Across All Parts of Speech
MAKS PANNER
September 2025
 

This article introduces the Reverse Entailment Diagnostic (RED), a novel bidirectional inference mechanism demonstrating that all parts of speech can be nominalized to occupy nounslot positions (e.g., subject, object) as syntactical noun entities, suggesting a universal noun slot in the linguistic mind. Using examples—“square/quadrilateral,” “to book carefully,” and “her house”— across English, Mandarin, and Tongan, I show how derived forms (e.g., possessives, nominalizations) entail backward to confirm nominal potential. RED challenges lexicalist frameworks like Transformational Generative Grammar, supporting dynamic models such as Distributed Morphology and Construction Grammar. Cross-linguistic evidence and cognitive implications underscore a universal cognitive capacity for nominalization, with applications in syntax, semantics, and natural language processing
