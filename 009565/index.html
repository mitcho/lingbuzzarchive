From Reverse Entailment Diagnostic to the Universal Linguistic Domain
MAKS PANNER
December 2025
 

The article argues that "RED (Reverse Entailment Diagnostic)" —a bidirectional test using nominalization/possessive packaging plus noun-slot verification—does more than show “anything can be nominalized.” If RED works systematically across categories and languages, it implies a stable **universal noun slot** (a referential position-type), and therefore a broader background structure: "ULD (Universal Linguistic Domain)", a geometrical space of "slot-families*"  and licensed transitions. ULD is derived as minimally containing five families: "referential" (entity tracking), "predicational" (property/event attribution), " operator" (determiners/quantifiers/TAM/negation), "linking"  (argument structure), and "packaging" (nominalization/embedding/coordination). The paper also integrates "exeme"  as pre-typed expression material that becomes a "lexeme by placement"  into ULD slots, not by prior association. It closes with testable neurocognitive predictions and computational applications (RED as weak supervision for slot/nominality detection in parsing and SRL).
