Addendum to the Mapbook of Syntactic Variation in American English: Survey Results, 2020–2021
Jim Wood, Guilherme Pereira
December 2025
 

This paper presents an addendum to the Mapbook of Syntactic Variation in American English (Wood et al. 2020), reporting the results of additional acceptability-judgment surveys conducted by the Yale Grammatical Diversity Project in 2020–2021. Using the same survey design, data-processing pipeline, and mapping techniques as the original mapbook, we extend the empirical record to a new set of syntactic constructions, including additional uses of hella, causal where, the alternative one construction, intensificational fully, and several cases of object omission with transitive verbs, which we call “lonely transitives”. As in the original work, we provide geospatial visualizations of sentence-level judgments alongside basic demographic breakdowns. This addendum is intended to be read in conjunction with the 2020 mapbook, which provides the full methodological, theoretical, and interpretive background for the data presented here.
