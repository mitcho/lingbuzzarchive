Towards the construction of a formal definition-classification system for cursion
Cai-Mei Yang
March 2025
 

Recursion in language is a hot topic in many fields like theoretical linguistics, psycholinguistics and neurolinguistics. However, there is no clear or consistent definition of recursion in previous studies. The current paper means to construct a formal definition-classification system for recursion in language, consisting of different subtypes of recursive rules and various models and levels of recursive sequences, exemplified with rules and sequences in English, Mandarin Chinese, etc. By doing so, the paper tries to unify some diverging definitions in the literature into a consistent system and alleviate some confusion that exists in the field.
