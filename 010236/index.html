Exploring 17th and 18th century Frisian. Syntactic and morphological observations
Eric Hoekstra
August 2026
 

This work is a collection of syntactic and morphological observations on the Frisian language of the 17th and 18th centuries. The observations presented here were collected as I read through all Frisian texts of this period for my two anthologies of Frisian literature of this period. If the Dutch language of these centuries is already severely understudied, one can imagine how much worse this is for a minority language like Frisian. The lay-out of the present work is quite simple. The observations are classified by syntactic category. The relevant example sentences are given, glossed, translated and it is briefly explained how these examples contrast with Modern Frisian. The first draft of this work was written in Frisian, but the present version was rewritten in English, because Frisian studies need all the help they can get, and who knows whether this work catches somebody’s fancy.
