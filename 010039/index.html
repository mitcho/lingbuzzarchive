Big DP & Big PRO: A unified account of reflexive and impersonal se
John David Storment, Francisco Ordóñez
May 2026
 

We argue that, despite the lack of an overt external argument in (most) impersonal/passive se clauses in Spanish, there is a syntactically projected external argument: an empty category PRO necessarily doubled by the pronominal clitic se. We unify all instances of so-called impersonal, passive and generic se as involving the same kind of external argument, whose interpretive qualities are determined by an operator. We further extend the same analysis to internal reflexive arguments that are also necessarily doubled by se, thus capturing the intuitive fact that PRO has anaphoric properties (Manzini 1983; Landau 2000; Kayne 2018; 2025). Our analysis has a major advantage, which is a uniform treatment of all the aforementioned instances of se as pronominal clitics that double the same type of argument. Our analysis thus avoids the need to posit complicated semantic values of different instances of se, and captures several distributional idiosyncrasies of the clitic se.
