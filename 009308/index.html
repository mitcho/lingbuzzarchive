Illicit LBE in Russian sluicing: rescue by deletion (of linearization statements)
Ivan Kalyakin, Daniar Kasenov
September 2025
 

This paper discusses a case of exceptional extraction from Russian PPs that only happens in sluicing / fragments. The proposed analysis rests on a version of repair by ellipsis pursued in the Cyclic Linearization literature (Fox, Pesetsky 2005; Davis 2021; Mendes, Kandybowicz 2023): illicit non-successive-cyclic movement is possible when the conflicting linearization statements are deleted via ellipsis. The analysis extends to other cases of exceptional extraction in Russian, when coupled with a Scattered Deletion analysis of LBE (Fanselow & Ćavar 2002, Bondarenko & Davis 2023). Finally, we discuss a number of overgeneration issues for our account.
