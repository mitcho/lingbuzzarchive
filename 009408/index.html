Who am I to (dis)agree? Interpretation Sensitive Agreement: Experimental evidence from French relatives
Isabelle Charnavel, Anouk Dieuleveut, Tom Meadows, David Mueller, Dominique Sportiche
October 2025
 

Agreement morphology is often assumed to realize phi-features uninterpreted at LF. We provide experimental evidence from French relative clauses showing that agreement can in fact have an interpretative effect, adding to the range of `semantic agreement' effects (e.g. Charnavel-Sportiche:2025: https://ling.auf.net/lingbuzz/009362). The choice of variable person agreement turns out to be sensitive to the placement of focus. We provide an analysis that relies on the interaction between focus, binding and the distribution of focus-sensitive operators.
