The Referential Shift of the Chinese “VP-de” Structures in Contrastive Contexts: A Sign-Based Construction Grammar Perspective
Liyong Wang
January 2026
 

This paper aims to establish a unified grammatical mechanism for the referential shift of “VP-de” structures in Modern Chinese within the framework of Sign-based Construction Grammar. It addresses the long-standing disjunction in previous studies between the mechanisms of semantic role determination and contextual regulation in the realization of referential shift. The focus is on how the contrastive construction, as a contextual device, facilitates the referential shift of “VP-de.” The central arguments are as follows: (1) in all “VP-de” structures, the referential target is always a frame element within the relevant cognitive frame; (2) when “VP-de” refers to agents or patients, the referential target corresponds to the verb’s arguments and valence elements, whereas when it refers to other elements, the target belongs to non-core frame elements; (3) contrastive constructions promote non-core frame elements to the status of Question Under Discussion (QUD) foci, thereby licensing them as referential targets. This study not only proposes a unified analytical approach to the referential shift of “VP-de” but also demonstrates the descriptive precision and predictive power of Sign-Based Construction Grammar, offering important insights for the analysis of Chinese constructions.
