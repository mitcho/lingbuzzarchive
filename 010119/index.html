Is it syntax or syncretism? Phases and honorific case markers in Korean
Soo-Hwan Lee
July 2026
 

This work develops a phase-based analysis of Korean honorific case markers. Korean displays an asymmetry in its case paradigm: while nominative, dative, and vocative case markers have their honorific counterparts, the accusative case marker does not. I argue that this gap is not accidental. Instead, it follows from phasehood in syntax. Adopting the view that vP, ApplP, and CP are phases (Chomsky 2000; 2001; McGinnis 2000; Ershova 2024), I propose that an honorific head (H) selects a phase and establishes agreement with the closest [+HUMAN] argument at the phase edge. This work demonstrates that syntax, rather than morphological syncretism, offers an explanation for the empirical puzzle at issue.
