The Pointing Role of Language
Oleksandr Naumenko
August 2025
 

Language is often mentioned as a means of communication and knowledge transfer. However, its components and mechanisms are designed for a different function, namely, pointing at relevant phenomena, relying on the cognitive abilities of the communication parties. This paper provides arguments in favor of that claim and explains how language machinery performs that role.
