How to talk about groups in a language without group nouns: the case of Passamaquoddy [handout]
Cooper Roberts, Haoming Li, Yizhen Jiang
November 2025
 

Passamaquoddy lacks group nouns. Instead, group interpretations are derived from plural nouns and free relatives, while group member reading is also available but requires plural agreement with the predicate and the preceding demonstrative. Further, when plural nouns are preceded by the universal quantifier i-pesq, both individual-distributive and group-distributive readings are available. We propose a covert group formation operator G to account for the above observations in Passamaquoddy.
Presented at TripleA 12, 10 September 2025.
