Merge and Principle C
Nirmalangshu Mukherji
May 2025
 

The human mind contains devices that generate unbounded representations in language, music, arithmetic etc. Since these devices were not adopted from pre-hominid species, we face an explanatory gap for the emergence of each device. We attempt to minimize the explanatory gap by exploring the prospect that a single generative device satisfies generativity for all domains. To that end, we study the generative device of language, Merge. It appears that the simplest form of Merge may obtain throughout the generative mind because (a) the formulation of Merge is not language-specific (b) Merge operates in arithmetic and music.
