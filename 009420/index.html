Specificity and “Non-Derived Environment Blocking” in Logical Phonology
Charles Reiss
November 2025
 

Despite Inkelas' (2000) instinct that such an analysis "would be far-fetched and uninsightful" I show how appeal to underspecification accounts for Turkish velar deletion with a simple, albeit counterintuitive, narrow phonological solution couched in Logical Phonology (LP). Components of the LP approach are introduced via discussion of Finnish assibilation, which, like the Turkish pattern, has also been discussed in terms of "non-derived environment blocking." New version has very minor corrections.
