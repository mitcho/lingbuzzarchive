Meaningful agreement features: evidence from indexical binding
Isabelle Charnavel, Tom Meadows, Dominique Sportiche
June 2025
 

We discuss the semantic import of number and person agreement features (on T, but with wider relevance to other agreeing head probes). We first illustrate that number/person features on T can encode a presupposition about the subject’s denotation. We then discuss the licensing interaction between agreement and indexical binding in French and English (documenting new restrictions on its availability) to support the hypothesis that person agreement can (and perhaps must) have semantic import.
