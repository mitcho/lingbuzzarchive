Cross-linguistic realization of Transitivity in proverbial structures: a thematic view
Soroor Karamloo
July 2025
 

This study compares 774 equivalent proverbs in English, Persian, and Spanish using Halliday’s (1994) Systemic Functional Grammar (SFG), focusing on the experiential and textual metafunctions. I selected 258 proverbs from each language using monolingual dictionaries and analyzed their clause structures in terms of process, participant, and circumstance along the Theme and Rheme of the clauses. Although researchers have applied SFG to comparative linguistics, they have not explored proverbs across these three languages. I hypothesize that equivalent proverbs share semantic and syntactic features despite cultural and structural differences. The analysis reveals that although similar trend is followed across the three corpora, English and Persian prove to be more similar syntactically and semantically in terms of participant types both in the Theme and Rheme.
