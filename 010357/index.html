A semantic analysis of Cantonese sin
Ka-Hin Ng
September 2026
 

In this thesis, we investigate the multifaceted uses of the Cantonese particle sin (先). Firstly, we argue with historical evidence that the temporal sin derives the question focus sin and the scalar sin separately. Secondly, we argue that the formulations of temporal sin derives question-focus by type-shifting: while temporal sin arranges the sequence of two propositions, question sin arranges the sequence of two questions. Then we argue that semantics of scalar sin gives a long presupposition between its prejacent p and a contextually salient expectation r. It combines with various exclusive particles by predicate modification. Decomposing the scalar and the exclusive components resolve the directionality puzzle: a single sin can associate with various exclusive particles, which can have either directionality.
