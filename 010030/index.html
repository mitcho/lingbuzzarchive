When concord meets suppletion: on double plural marked adjectives in Ingush
Philip Shushurin
June 2026
 

This paper examines the patterns of apparent double plural marking on the Ingush adjective -oaqqa/-oaqqii ‘big’. It presents evidence against Norris’s (2022) analysis of the non-prefixal plural marking on this adjective as concord. New data demonstrate that (a) prefixal and non-prefixal plurality may mismatch, and (b) such mismatches are disallowed in certain idiomatic contexts. The alternation -oaqqa/-oaqqii is treated as an instance of number suppletion and is analyzed as word-external contextual allomorphy. The paper discusses whether – and to what extent – the Ingush data in question can be seen as evidence against analyzing adjectival number suppletion as a concord-mediated operation.
