Beyond thematic uniqueness
Mathieu Paillé
December 2025
 

A common way of characterising the relationship between events and their participants is to postulate that the latter are assigned thematic roles. This is subject to a 'thematic uniqueness' constraint: an event cannot have more than one participant bearing the same thematic role. Building on recent work on predicates of individuals, this article demonstrates that thematic uniqueness is part of a broader phenomenon, which I call 'predicational uniqueness.' I model predicational uniqueness as stemming from predicational exhaustification; on this view, all predicates, whether they are thematic predicates of events or non-thematic predicates of individuals, are locally strengthened vis-à-vis alternative predicates from the same conceptual or thematic class.
