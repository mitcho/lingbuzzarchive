Wh-movement paths and oblique extraction in Mam (Mayan)
Noah Elkins, Harold Torrence, Colin Brown
August 2026
 

This paper examines the Ā-extraction of adjuncts in San Juan Ostuncalco Mam (SJO Mam; Mayan), focusing on the enclitic =(y)a’, which (optionally) appears on predicates when certain adjuncts are extracted. We describe the distribution of =(y)a’ in monoclausal and long-distance extraction and show that, with the exception of temporals, the extraction adjuncts licenses the enclitic, which may occur multiple times along a movement dependency, including within a single clause. We argue that =(y)a’ provides a morphological footprint of the path taken by an extracted adjunct. Specifically, we analyze =(y)a’ as the spellout of Ā-agreement between the extracted adjunct and intermediate heads along the clausal spine, providing evidence that Ā-movement proceeds successive-cyclically through the extended verbal domain, both within and across clauses.
