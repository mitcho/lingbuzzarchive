Two types of exclusive doubling in Vietnamese
Ka-Fai Yip
September 2026
 

This study focuses on the syntax-semantic mismatches arising from the doubling of exclusive focus particles ‘only’ in Vietnamese: (i) adverbial chỉ with adfocal mỗi and with (ii) sentence-final particle (SFP) thôi. Despite multiple occurrences, only one particle is interpreted as the exclusive operator. Following the operator-particle approach, I propose that only adverbial chỉ is the operator, which establishes different syntactic dependencies with mỗi and thôi that are truth-conditionally void: (i) covert movement triggered by focus [FOC] features in adverbial-adfocal doubling, and (ii) syntactic Agree with exclusive [EXCL] features in adverbial-SFP doubling. The non-uniformity is supported by their distinct locality profiles and (non-)sensitivity to intervention by quantificational elements. Furthermore, I argue that focus and quantificational elements constitute different featural classes for minimality-based intervention.
