Was Pierre Marie right? Not really
Stefano Cappa, Eleonora Catricalà
September 2024
 

Pierre Marie, the arch-enemy of Jules Déjerine and Augusta Klumpke-Déjerine, is apparently enjoying a new wave of popular- ity (Coutinho, Caramelli & Teive 2021, Weiller, Glauche & Rijntjes 2022) . While some of his anatomical work may be justly appreciated, a careful reading of his work on aphasia indicates that his theoretical stance was deeply flawed.
