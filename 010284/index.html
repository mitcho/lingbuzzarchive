3^n segments and 3^n natural classes, each of cardinality 3^k
Charles Reiss
September 2026
 

I defend the following three claims under the assumptions of Logical Phonology: Universal Grammar makes available an inventory of 3^n segments; there are 3^n natural classes; and the cardinality of each natural class is 3^k, 1 ≤ k ≤ n.
