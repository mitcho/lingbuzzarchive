The Morphosyntax of Possessive Pronouns and Silent Nominals in Mongolian
Chigchi Bai
March 2026
 

This paper studies the morphosyntax of Mongolian possessive pronouns within the framework of Distributed Morphology. The core proposal is that possessive pronouns are realizations of formal feature clusters that are generated by fusion.
