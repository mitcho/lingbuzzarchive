ENGAGE Framework: A Next-Generation Architecture Complementing ChatGPT for Enhanced Conversational AI
Dr SenthilKumar Anantharaman
August 2025
 

The rapid advancement of large language models such as OpenAI’s ChatGPT has revolutionized natural language generation and conversational AI. However, the inherent limitations of standalone generative models in managing user engagement, contextual continuity, adaptation, and guided interactions necessitate a more comprehensive framework. This paper proposes the AGE (Automata Generator Evolver) framework, which generates and evolves automata models to complement ChatGPT. AGE integrates formal state-based representations and evolutionary optimization to enhance dialogue management, prompt engineering, and explainability. In this paper, we detail the framework architecture, automata representations, evolutionary algorithms, and potential applications. Design diagrams illustrating the framework components and workflow are provided. This hybrid approach offers promising improvements in controllability and interpretability for conversational AI systems. Further this paper proposes the ENGAGE framework, an architectural paradigm, designed to complement and extend ChatGPT’s capabilities. ENGAGE introduces modular components for Engagement, Natural language understanding, Generation, Adaptation, Guidance, and Evolution, enabling more dynamic, personalized, and context-aware human-AI interaction systems. Finally, we present the design principles, architecture, and potential applications of ENGAGE, highlighting its role in evolving conversational agents toward deeper user engagement and continuous improvement
