A Preliminary Phonemic Analysis of Haryanvi
Madan Mohan
March 2026
 

This paper presents a phonemic analysis of the West-Central variety of Haryanvi spoken in parts of Bhiwani and the Hansi region of Hisar district in Haryana, India. Based on minimal pair evidence and native speaker data, the study identifies an inventory of thirty-two consonant phonemes, nine vowel phonemes, and three diphthongs. The study also examines syllable structure and the phonotactic distribution of segments. The findings contribute to the phonological description of Haryanvi, which remains relatively under-documented in linguistic literature.
