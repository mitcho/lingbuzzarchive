Local prominence in micro information packagings
Yoshihisa Kitagawa
January 2024
 

The goal of this work is to demonstrate the existence of what can be characterized as "micro" information packaging independent of the notions of "macro" information packaging. In particular, I will attempt to motivate the notion 'local prominence,' which is established within each syntactic projection independent of such notions as focus-background, topic-comment and new/given information. The existence of local prominence is demonstrated when we observe that the shift of its semantico-pragmatic interpretation is synchronized with the shift of a prosodic pattern in Tokyo Japanese. It is also pointed out that what has been regarded as Metrical Boost in fact is a prosodic reflex of informational prominence that is iteratively taking place in a larger syntactic construction.
