Constraints on modal flavor colexification
Jeremy Kuhn, Amira Ramdani
October 2025
 

Modal items are known to often show ambiguity with respect to modal flavor. For example, English 'can' can express both deontic and circumstantial flavor. To date, however, it remains unstudied whether there are particular sets of modal flavors that are more frequently colexified than others across the world’s languages. Here, we avail ourself of the recent database of Uegaki et al. (2024) to uncover natural classes of modal flavor colexification, and to evaluate the degree that these natural classes can be captured using different kinds of ontological structures.
