Tier‑ Nested Shell Composition for Nominal Operator Architecture A unified syntactic shell model integrating MGD, HTQA tier nesting, and RED/DED traversal
MAKS PANNER
January 2026
 

This chapter specifies a syntactic (functional) shell‑ composition architecture for the nominal phrase and aligns it with three programmatic components developed in the companion manuscripts: (i) Modifiability‑ Graph Diagnostics (MGD) for tier assignment, (ii) a ranked operator/quantifier architecture (HTQA tier nesting) for closure and traversal, and (iii) a bidirectional RED/DED traversal methodology for diagnosing packaging dependencies. The architectural commitment is deliberately minimal and strictly tier‑ based: Shell 1 hosts LOW primitives; Shell 2 hosts MIDDLE packaging/repair orbitals; Shell 3 hosts HIGH operator ranks; Shell 4 hosts connective bridges; Shell 5 hosts outer polarity and outer minimizers that scope over the assembled closure stack. The model is evaluated not by category labels alone but by graph position and closure sensitivity: shells are empirically testable via directed head‑ modification graphs and by explicit falsification templates. The chapter integrates cross‑ linguistic packaging strategies (article, article‑ less, classifier systems) and provides a filled‑ slot inventory that is intentionally extensible
