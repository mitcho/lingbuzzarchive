Language Attitudes: Finnish University Students’ Attitudes Toward Accented English
Zhang Zixian
April 2025
 

This study investigates Finnish university students’ attitudes toward accented English, specifically comparing Russian-accented English (RE) and light-accented English (LE). Using the matched-guise technique (MGT), this study collected data from 12 Finnish university students who rated a single female speaker in two distinct accents in English. Subjects evaluated each accent across traits related to solidarity and status. Results indicate that in terms of status-related traits, LE and RE make no significant differences, except that LE received significantly more positive evaluations than RE in the education trait, while RE was perceived as more sincere, showing greater solidarity than LE. Contrary to the initial hypotheses, which anticipated negative attitudes toward RE in both status and solidarity-related traits, given the current geopolitical tensions and international images of Russia. Data reveal nuanced perceptual differences between LE and RE, with clear distinctions in perceived socioeconomic backgrounds linked to the accents.
