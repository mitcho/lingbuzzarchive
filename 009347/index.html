Selective grammatical encoding of emotions in language
Guglielmo Cinque
September 2024
 

In Cinque 2013 I considered one potential argument for Universal Grammar. One stemming from the limited number of functional notions that are grammatically encoded in the languages of the world. What we find is that only a fraction of our cognitive concepts and distinctions seems to receive a grammatical encoding. Here I focus on the specific domain of emotions.
