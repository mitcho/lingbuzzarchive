Absolutive agreement failure and the flavors of T/Infl in Mam
Noah Elkins
March 2026
 

This squib analyzes matrix clauses with no overt aspect morpheme in Mam, a Mayan language of Guatemala and Mexico. These clauses involve systematic failure of absolutive (Set B) agreement with intransitive subjects and transitive objects; if either is pronominal, must be expressed overtly in argument position (i.e. not pro-dropped). I argue that these clauses involve a phonologically null completive aspect morpheme (Ø, glossed 'NCOM'), which realizes a T/Infl head that idiosyncratically lacks a phi-probe. Evidence is presented that this agreement failure is not reducible to the phonological status of either the aspect morpheme itself or the Set B morphemes (cf. Woolford 2011). I propose that Set B agreement in Mayan must therefore be disentangled from both argument licensing and object-over-subject inversion, the latter of which is independently required in high-absolutive Mayan languages like Mam (Ranero 2021, Scott 2023).
