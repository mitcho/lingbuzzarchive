Factors for hyperactivity in Tigrinya
Jason Overfelt
August 2025
 

This paper explores the mechanisms responsible for the hyperactivity of embedded nominal arguments in Tigrinya (Ethiosemitic; SOV). Building on current trends, I argue that patterns of multiple A-dependencies across clause boundaries are most plausibly motivated by the need to satisfy formal requirements of verbal functional heads. This is in opposition to more traditional theories of (non-)hyperactivity that appeal either to properties of the hyperactive nominal, including Case and agreement, or to deficiencies of syntactic domains.
