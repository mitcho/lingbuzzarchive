II. The PP in Old Frisian
Eric Hoekstra
September 2026
 

This is part II of the book: "Explorations in Old Frisian Syntax". It contains observations about prepositions, postpositions and verb particles in Old Frisian. The book consists of five parts, which have been uploaded separately. The five parts are: I. A Broad Outline of Old Frisian II. The PP in Old Frisian III. The NP in Old Frisian IV. The VP in Old Frisian V. The AP in Old Frisian
