Phonologically conditioned affix order in Yidiɲ: Evidence for strata
Marie-Luise Popp
March 2025
 

This paper discusses the non-uniform behavior of different affixes in phonologically conditioned affix order in Yidiɲ. I present evidence from final syllable deletion illustrating that the word comprises two different cyclic domains. Phonologically conditioned affix order is bound to the first cyclic domain, hence being counter-fed by affixes of the second layer. This study further substantiates the model of the morphologyphonology interface as proposed by Stratal Optimality Theory, which posits temporary phonological access to morpheme boundaries, predicting phonological conditioned affix order to operate within one stratum.
