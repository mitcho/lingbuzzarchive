Controller shift in centered-world semantics
Wataru Uegaki
February 2011
 

This is a very old handout on controller shift (from 2011). Per request from colleagues who wish to cite the work, I am putting it available online. -- Wataru Uegaki === What would be a semantic account of control, which can capture the different controller choices, including the controller shift phenomenon? What do control facts tell us about the semantics of attitude reports? In this talk, I try to address these questions. I argue that controller choice is constrained in two independent ways: (i) De se requirement that the individual argument of the complement (i.e. PRO) must correspond to an attitude center; (ii) The matrix controller argument corresponds to the embedded agent. The facts about controller choice provide a new piece of evidence for the centered-world analysis of control.
