R-Anchors and the Architecture of Argumenthood
Tariq Nazar
January 2026
 

This paper develops a minimal architectural reanalysis of argumenthood. It proposes that predicates project abstract dependency objects — 'R-anchors' — that encode the existence of argument-related dependencies independently of their overt realization and referential identification. Argumenthood emerges only when such dependencies are structurally completed by a single operation — 'Anchor-Checking'. On this view, omission, generic interpretation, and persistent ambiguity are licensed outcomes of grammatical structure rather than exceptional repairs.
