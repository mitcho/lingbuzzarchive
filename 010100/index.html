Phonological and syntactic locality of object clitics in Czech Evidence from external sandhi
Markéta Ziková
February 2026
 

This preprint of the chapter (included in the monograph Czech Clitics: New Insights and Perspectives) examines the phonological aspects of cliticisation, focusing on how phonological and syntactic locality influence the phonological integration of second-position object clitics with a preceding host. Integration is assessed through processes affecting voicing at the object–clitic boundary, namely final obstruent devoicing and pre-sonorant voicing. These external sandhi processes are investigated using reading tasks with Czech speakers, whose readings were recorded and analysed in Praat. The experimental results reveal a statistically significant difference between verbal and adverbial hosts, which we attribute to the fact that only the former establish a local configuration with object clitics.
