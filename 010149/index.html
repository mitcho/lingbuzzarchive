From Quotation to Concession: The Case of East Circassian
Ksenia Ershova, Itamar Francez
April 2026
 

This paper presents and analyzes a previously undescribed concessive modifier in East Circassian which is headed by an adverbial form of a quotative verb. This adverbial modifier is used, roughly,to express speaker dispreference for a potential action attributed by the speaker to the quoted agent. We show that this concessive modifier is subject to a set of restrictions on what it can modify, the most striking of which is a restriction to modifiands with negative directive sentential force. We outline a semantic analysis of this adverbial that explains the restrictions on its distribution in terms of two kinds of non-at-issue contents that conspire to generate a concessive interpretation. Our discussion situates the adverbial within broader discussions of concessivity and quotative verbs.
