An anthropic principle in lieu of a "Universal Grammar"
Hubert Haider
September 2019
 

An analogue of the cosmological Anthropic Principle in combination with the Darwinian theory of evolution applied to replicative cognitive programmes languages is sufficient for explaining fundamental grammatical properties as a result of the predictable convergent cognitive evolution of their grammars. "Universal Grammar" is a misperception of the results of cognitve evolution on shaping grammar systems.
