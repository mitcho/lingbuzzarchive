Nombres propios determinados y gramática del registro. Observaciones desde la Romania Nova
Samara Almeida, Andrés Saab
February 2026
 

The aim of this work is to offer a comparative analysis of the construction of [article + proper noun] in Río de la Plata Spanish (e.g., "la Samara") and Brazilian Portuguese (e.g., "a Samara"). Despite the different distribution observed in both varieties, the article is neither a mere expletive nor the iota operator that the same form of the article realizes in definite descriptions. On the contrary, the article indicates an expressive syntax and semantics, in the sense of multidimensional semantics (Potts 2005, McCready 2010, 2019, Gutzmann 2015, 2019, and Saab and Carranza 2021, among others). Specifically, the article signals a certain interpersonal proximity relationship between the referent of the proper name and the discourse participants (Oggiani and Aguilar Guevara 2024). Assuming that certain aspects of social meanings are entirely derived by properties of PF, we propose that the expressive article is the byproduct of a doubling rule referring to the phonetic form of nominal roots. The fact that the article is more ubiquitous with first names in some varieties of Brazilian Portuguese follows as familiar process of grammaticalization, which, in this case, is sensitive to the register properties of nominal phrases containing proper names.
