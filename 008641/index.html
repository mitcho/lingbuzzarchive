De re readings of the Lobi logophoric pronoun: results and diagnostics
Shweta Akolkar, Sansan Claude Sansan Claude Hien
November 2024
 

It is debated whether logophoric pronouns across languages are obligatorily construed de se. Much of the debate has centered on a single language, Ewe (Pearson 2015, Bimpeh 2019, Bimpeh 2023, Bassi et al. 2023, i.a.). This study reports on the finding that the logophoric pronoun in Lobi, another West African language, is compatible with de re readings. We reflect on the methodological recommendations in earlier works for de re testing and suggest that they may introduce biases and unwanted readings. In light of this, we offer new methodological suggestions.
