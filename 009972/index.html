PP-complexes and shell structure
Malhaar Shah, Tonia Bleam
August 2026
 

In this paper, we consider cases where there are, on the surface, two (or more) adjacent PPs, like "across the mall from the library", or "past the goalkeeper into the net". We argue that the grammar allows two distinct derivations for two adjacent PPs: one in which the PPs are both independent VP modfiers, and one where they form a constituent before modifying the VP. For some PP-pairs like "across NP from NP", we argue that only the latter derivation is available. Our view has as a notable consequence that there can be a one-to-many relationship between meanings and syntactic derivations. In addition, we demonstrate that the facts are compatible with a binary-branching model of grammar.
