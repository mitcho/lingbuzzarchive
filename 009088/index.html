Beyond prediction: comments on the format of natural intelligence
Elliot Murphy
June 2025
 

I offer suggestions for empirical expansion and conceptual refinement with respect to Parr et al.’s (2025) promising framework. The authors discuss sequential rules, but how might we speak of the non-sequential, hierarchical and recursive properties of human language that cannot – in principle – be reduced to statistical, subsymbolic information? I outline some ways in which active inference could potentially implement these concerns of hierarchical constituent geometry.
