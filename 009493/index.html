The non-identity reading in Slavic ATB constructions
Petr Biskup
November 2025
 

This paper presents Across-the-Board (ATB) constructions with the non-identity reading in Slavic languages that have not received much attention so far. It shows that the data pose a problem for most current approaches, not only for approaches designed to derive the identity reading of ATB constructions but also for approaches that assume the existence of the non-identity interpretation. The article proposes an analysis with multiple movement and a distinctness requirement on linearization in ATB constructions. It also identifies several factors relevant to determining the type of the reading of the moved element. Mostly, the factors can be subsumed under definiteness effects.
