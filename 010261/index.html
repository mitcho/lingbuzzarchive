A constraint on quantification over parts (the gather/numerous distinction)
Omri Amiraz
August 2026
 

This paper investigates why some predicates are compatible with quantification over parts of singular individuals, while others are not. For example, Most of the trail is narrow is acceptable, but #Most of the trail is short is not. I propose a constraint on quantification over parts, which derives the contrast between quantification-compatible and quantification-resistant predicates from an independently motivated pragmatic principle banning contextual equivalence among scalar alternatives. This analysis explains when and why quantification over parts of singular individuals gives rise to infelicity, and it naturally extends to the phenomenon of quantification compatibility in collective predication, known as the gather/numerous distinction.
