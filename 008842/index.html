Incidencia de efectos semánticos en el procesamiento sintáctico: un abordaje desde las ambigüedades de adjunción
Noelia Ayelén Stetie
February 2025
 

In this doctoral dissertation, I investigate the interaction between semantic and syntactic processes during sentence comprehension, with a particular focus on attachment ambiguities in Rioplatense Spanish. Grounded in the framework of experimental psycholinguistics, my research explores how semantic information, especially that carried by verbs, influences the establishment of syntactic relations in sentences with ambiguous relative clauses. I also address the broader debate on universality versus variability in language processing by examining a variety of Spanish that has received limited attention in psycholinguistic research. The methodological approach includes six experiments involving over 1000 participants with diverse sociolinguistic profiles. Results reveal that semantic cues significantly affect syntactic parsing, with varying degrees of influence depending on verb type and syntactic structure. These findings challenge strictly modular models of language processing by supporting hybrid accounts that consider algorithmic and heuristics processing routes. My research contributes original empirical data to the understanding of how linguistic and extralinguistic factors interact in real-time sentence comprehension. By focusing on a less-studied variety of Spanish, my doctoral dissertation enriches cross-linguistic perspectives on sentence processing and offers insights for refining theoretical models in psycholinguistics.
