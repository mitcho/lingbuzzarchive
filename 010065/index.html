A Sketch of the Sampang Language
J. Joseph Perry
June 2026
 

This work is a grammatical sketch of the Sampang language, an endangered language belonging to the Kiranti branch of the Trans‑Himalayan (Sino‑Tibetan) language family. It is the first published work to cover all aspects of grammar, including phonology, morphology, syntax and lexicon. Like many Kiranti languages, Sampang has extremely complex verbal morphology, displaying both subject and object agreement as well as intricate morphophonological alternations, and a substantial portion of the sketch is devoted to these issues. Other points of linguistic interest include the syntax of case and agreement, and the natureof linking morphs in the language. Some notes are made on the lexicon of the language, which displays thoroughgoing contact phenomena and an unusually rich array of motion verbs.
