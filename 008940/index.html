(Quasi-)indexical tense: on temporal interpretation of Kazym Khanty embedded clauses
Daria Sidorkina
November 2024
 

This paper describes a temporal system of Kazym Khanty and shows how non-finite tense is constrained in habituals and propositional attitudes. I suggest that emerging temporal patterns of Kazym Khanty can be explained by assuming that tenses function as logophoric pronouns, or indexiphors in terms of Deal (2018, 2020), and using introducing the anchor time as an interpretation parameter. This assumption is supported by observations that tenses differ from temporal indexical adverbials in their shifting behaviour and obey the Locality and Shift Together constraints.
