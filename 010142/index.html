Fonology: Phonological analysis in R
Guilherme Duarte Garcia
July 2026
 

Fonology is an R package for phonological analysis, with tools for grapheme-to-phoneme conversion, syllabification, stress assignment, sonority-based measures, natural-class feature matrices, and maximum entropy modeling. It provides primary support for Portuguese, Spanish, French, and Italian, and English transcription that allows user-correctable overrides and heuristic fallback for out-of-vocabulary forms. Accuracy of grapheme-phoneme conversion ranges from 94% to 99% (using a combination of lookup and regex). Functions are vectorized, which yields fast performance over large texts (fast for R, that is). The package is designed to support reproducible phonological research and teaching by integrating common analytical workflows into a consistent R interface, including transcription, feature-based generalization, quantitative phonotactic measures, and visualization-ready outputs.
