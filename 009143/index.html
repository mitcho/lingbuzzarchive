Thematic reversals in stative non-passives
Lefteris Paparounas
June 2025
 

DPs appearing in stative passives are typically read as themes of the stativized event. This paper tackles cases -- dubbed thematic reversals -- that appear to constitute exceptions to this pattern: in some cases, the DP of which a stative passive is predicated is apparently read as the agent of the entailed event. The paper uses such cases as a window onto the argument structure of the stative passive: in particular, the fact that thematic reversals take place in stative passives, but never in their eventive counterparts, shows that the label ‘stative passive’ is a misnomer, with these structures being instances of external predication. The results bear more broadly on the question of how to circumscribe the range of DPs that felicitously appear in stative passives.,
