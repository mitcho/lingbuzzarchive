A Note on the Apparent Polarity Status of English Bare Much
Richard Kayne
September 2026
 

The apparent negative polarity behavior seen with 'much' and 'many' is a side effect of a licensing requirement holding of the silent AMOUNT or NUMBER that they cooccur with, much as in Kayne (2021) on 'long'. The overall conclusion is that negative polarity is not a primitive of the language faculty, at least not in the kind of cases discussed. Silent elements and their licensing requirements are more fundamental.
