Origins of Language Introduction
Keyur Joshi
February 2025
 

This work is about how language emerged. Language emerged as verbal communications long before it was written down. Mother of all human languages was a spoken language. It emerged in Homo Erectus, if not before. Spoken Language emerged with single phoneme words. These single phonemes words evolved into multi-phoneme words. Words evolved into Vibhakty/Case. They both together evolved into grammar. That evolved and branched into full fledge languages. The roots of language evolution *were* forgotten. But history is not lost. Original root meanings of single phoneme words can be traced and deciphered from from ancient work named Ashtadhyayi, that is compilation of even more ancient works. I have deciphered these meanings of original single phoneme words. More work is needed to improve degree of certainty and level of confidence and to fine tune meanings. I am sharing this work before it is complete, so that it does not get lost again. Someone can take it forward in case I fail.
