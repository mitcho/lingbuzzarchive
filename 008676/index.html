External possession in Kipsigis involves movement
Maria Kouneli
December 2024
 

In this paper, I provide an analysis of external possession constructions in Kipsigis (Nilotic; Kenya), and I argue that possessor raising best captures the properties of the phenomenon in the language. Evidence for movement comes from an interesting interaction between possessor raising and topicalization. My analysis highlights that well-known constraints on remnant movement can be used as diagnostics for movement in external possession constructions in various languages.
