Conflict, Policy and Threats to Linguistic Ecologies in the Caucasus
Thomas Wier
September 2025
 

This paper argues that language loss and endangerment, especially in multiethnic conflict zones like the Caucasus, are not incidental outcomes of conflict but are structured by the same forces that drive displacement and institutional breakdown. Drawing on case studies from Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan, I examine how political violence and public policy reconfigure linguistic ecologies through demographic shocks, forced migration, and state-led cultural assimilation.
