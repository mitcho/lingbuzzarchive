The Distribution of Wide Scope Indefinite Bare Duals in Kazym Khanty: a QUD-based Approach
Fedor Golosov
September 2025
 

This paper is aimed at accounting for the limited distribution of the wide scope existential (WSE) uses of bare dual NPs in Kazym Khanty. The author argues that the reason for this limitation is pragmatic in nature: while bare duals are existential quantifiers and the ability to get wide scope is always semantically available, some WSE uses are independently blocked by a QUD-based constraint. Namely, in such cases, bare duals are infelicitous because they are forced to address a QUD that targets their not-at-issue content, namely the duality meaning. The case of Kazym Khanty bare duals shows that absence of a WSE reading in a particular sentence does not suggest that the tested NP can not take variable scope and that the QUD parameter should be controlled for to avoid a “false negative” result.
