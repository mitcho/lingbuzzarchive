Reconstruction and linearization in wh-movement of DPs
Shrayana Haldar
April 2026
 

This paper argues that reconstruction for variable binding in wh-movement (e.g., Which picture of herself1 did every girl1 find?) should be analyzed fully on a par with pied-piping (e.g., whose book did John read?), and pied-piping, in turn, should be analyzed as consisting of two separate steps of movement: one semantically consequential step of movement that feeds question semantics, and one semantically vacuous step of movement the purpose of which is to simply pronounce a wh-containing constituent in the left periphery. When such a view of pied-piping is married with a view of movement which makes room for loosening the constraints on it, the result is what is usually described as reconstruction for variable binding in wh-movement. This approach is superior to one in which picture of herself is deleted in its higher position at LF, in two ways. Firstly, it sidesteps a weakness in the LF deletion approach (LF deletion must be constrained to overt movement and cannot apply to covert movement). Secondly, because of how pied-piping is packaged with the rest of the system, specific patterns in reconstruction can be accounted for, which existing theories of pied-piping/wh-movement are unable to explain.
