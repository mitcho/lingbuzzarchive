Stylistic applicatives: a lens into the nature of anticausative SE
Carlos Muñoz Pérez
September 2025
 

This paper examines an understudied syntactic phenomenon in Chilean Spanish: stylistic applicatives. This construction involves the appearance of the form LE alongside dative clitics in marked anticausative contexts. The pattern displays several puzzling properties, the most striking being that the anticausative/reflexive marker SE becomes optional in it, i.e., its presence or absence does not affect the interpretation of the sentence. I argue that this optionality arises from two interconnected factors. First, stylistic applicatives involve multiple exponence of an applicative head, realized as two separate clitic forms. Second, one of the resulting exponents, which is formally similar to a reflexive clitic, is able to replace SE in the surface representation of the sentence. This analysis supports the view that anticausative SE is a semantically vacuous marker whose role is purely formal: to signal the absence of an external argument in anticausative constructions.
