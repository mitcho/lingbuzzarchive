Quasi-names in Akan: A further piece of the definiteness puzzle
Carla Bombi, Dinah Adom Mac-Arthur
February 2026
 

This paper provides an analysis of definite bare nouns in Akan as quasi-names – nouns which, like "Mum" in English, can be used as names but are associated with descriptive content (Yip et al., 2023). The paper also provides further evidence that the Akan definite "no" is used in typical uniqueness contexts. Taken together, the data are consistent with an analysis of "no" as a uniqueness definite, whose reduced distribution can be explained by competition with the (quasi-name) bare noun (Bombi, 2018; Bombi et al., 2019).
