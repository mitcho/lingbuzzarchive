Defocus in Match Theory: Remarks on Yip & Yuan 2025
Nick Kalivoda
November 2025
 

Yip & Yuan (2025) add a new case to the literature on syntax-prosody mismatches and analyze it in terms of Match Theory with a novel constraint Defocus. In line with recent developments in syntax-prosody mapping in OT emphasizing the need to take all candidates and all constraints into account, I construct an OT system based on their proposals and show that the desired optima tie with unwanted competitors. This problem is resolved in an expanded system, but only by maintaining stipulations about prosodic headedness and nonterminal correspondence, removal of which results in harmonic bounding of the desired optima. I demonstrate that this problem too can be resolved, again by defining a full OT system and calculating its factorial typology. The discussion illustrates how proceeding from system to system, as required in modern rigorous OT, is necessary to advance theoretical inquiry.
