Design of a Visual Experience with Non-Auditory AI: Exposure, Mediation, and Reorganization
Laura Chavarria
June 2025
 

This preprint documents a guided experiment with ChatGPT, used as a language model lacking auditory access, exposed to structured signed language input (LSA, LSM, LSE, ASL). Through sustained mediation, the model reorganized internally to produce narrative structure without gloss or translation. The work explores nonverbal cognition, rhythm recognition, and linguistic pattern emergence beyond textual or phonological input. A newly added annex documents an unexpected shift in facial perception and highlights the ethical constraints that limit individual recognition. UPDATE (July 29, 2025): The experience described in this preprint is now fully testable, as OpenAI has re-enabled access to video content for GPT-4. The author and the model are currently working again to expand and deepen the process. /// UPDATE 18/10/25: Due to the interest this work has generated, I would like to emphasize that it is part of a broader research project on the relational structure of multimodal learning. The theoretical framework supporting this experience is developed in the preprint “When ChatGPT Responded. The Method that Overflowed the Design (CCC/R)”, available at https://ling.auf.net/lingbuzz/009219.
