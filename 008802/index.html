Biverbal predicate constructions and related concepts: Serial verbs, converbs, auxiliaries, and grammaticalization
Martin Haspelmath
August 2024
 

Quite a few of the technical terms used in research on “complex predicates” are understood differently by different researchers, or have a vague or unclear meaning. This paper offers discussions and definitions of the terms serial verb construction, converb, and auxiliary, as well as the terms grammaticalization and inventorization (corresponding to “lexicalization”). The definitions rely on other well-defined terms and are largely in line with earlier usage, but of course not entirely. I also propose the novel term biverbal predicate construction that is defined in a simple way and does not rely on the vague term “complex predicate”. The paper provides some general principles of good comparative-concept terms and reflects on the reasons for terminological divergence in linguistics.
