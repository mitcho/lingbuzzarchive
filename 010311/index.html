Variable-flavor attitude reports without underspecification
Jéssica Mendes, Giovanni Roversi
September 2026
 

We examine an apparent case of a variable-flavor attitude predicate, the verb kä in Äiwoo, which can receive ‘think’, ‘say’, or ‘want’ translations. We argue that this verb denotes linguistic production, which may be mental or physical (thus capturing its use in doxastic and assertive reports). Its use for bouletic reports arises from combining it with an overt priority modal (the prefix nä-) in the embedded clause, whose semantic contribution can be independently established from its use in unembedded contexts.
