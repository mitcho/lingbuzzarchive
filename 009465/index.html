PRO as an anaphoric variable: Rebinding and MAXELIDE-effects
Jason Overfelt
November 2025
 

This paper develops an argument in support of the claim that the embedded subject position of Control constructions is an anaphoric variable of the type represented by PRO. The evidence is based on an observation by Sag (1976) that, in the context of A'-movement, there is a preference in Control structures for ellipsis of the matrix predicate over ellipsis of the embedded predicate. I argue that this observation falls under the rubric of what are referred to as MAXELIDE-effects. This characterization of the data motivates an analysis whereby the rebinding of PRO disrupts ellipsis within the embedded clause. This behavior mirrors what we observe with overt pronouns, supporting the search for a shared explanation. While the majority of the paper deals with cases of Obligatory Control, I show that the analysis can be extended to account for MAXELIDE-effects with Non-Obligatory Control as well. I also discuss some of the complications of attempting a direct comparison of PRO with A-traces. However, I offer several considerations against appealing to the alternative A-movement analysis of Control to account for MAXELIDE-effects.
