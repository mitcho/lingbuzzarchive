Pugacheva meets the Binding Theory: proxy readings in Russian
Ruslan Rodionov
April 2025
 

This experimental study investigates the acceptability of proxy readings of Russian reflexives, contrasting affixal -sja (e.g., pomyl-sja 'washed') and simplex sebja (e.g., pomyl sebja 'washed himself').
