Root Duality: Unifying Indexical Identity and Computational Anchoring
CHANG kangjie
November 2025
 

This paper analyzes the study of root properties in constructional morphology, examining the multilingual process of complex word construction. Building on Belder & Craenenbroeck (2015), which characterizes the essence of roots as the “empty position” created by initial Merge-driven displacement, the paper presents a systematic analysis and argues that roots exhibit a dual nature as both indexical units and computational anchors.
