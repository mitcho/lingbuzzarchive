Degree QUDs and focus-sensitive scalar particles: Chinese dōu, cái, and jiù
Linmin Zhang
January 2026
 

Based on a degree-QUD perspective, this chapter analyzes Chinese "dōu", "cái", and "jiù" as "even"-like particles. Similar to English "even", these particles invoke a contextually salient degree QUD, ordering the focus associate's alternative set by informativeness, and requiring their prejacent to be maximally informative. "Dōu" vs. "cái/jiù" differ in their information projection patterns (order-preserving vs. order-reversing). Under this proposal, the two elements identified in the canonical analysis of “even”, i.e., a likelihood scale and entity-based additivity, are replaced by a degree-QUD-based informativeness scale and additivity along this scale, both reflected in the norm-sensitive inferences of these particles. This chapter demonstrates how this theoretical perspective yields new empirical insights and how cross-linguistic data contribute to theory development.
