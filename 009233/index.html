What's in a parasitic gap?
Matthew Hewett
August 2025
 

Prior literature has debated whether multiple gaps in parasitic gap constructions have a shared antecedent or separate antecedents. I adduce novel arguments for a shared antecedent approach from connectivity effects in English: parasitic gaps obligatorily match the binding theoretic class and syntactic category of the filler. I develop an analysis of parasitic gaps that derives these facts by synthesizing two very different previous accounts—Nissenbaum 2000 and Nunes 2004—in a way that preserves key insights of both. My analysis also clarifies the source of ‘weakest crossover’ effects with parasitic gaps, establishes novel tests for identifying null operators, and paves the way for a novel account of the A-/Ā-distinction in parasitic gap licensing.
