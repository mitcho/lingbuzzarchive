The Reconstruction of Qieyun Sibilants and Their Retention in Chinese Dialects
Yanguang炎光 Guo郭
August 2026
 

The mainstream reconstruction of Qieyun sibilants—specifically the jing (精) group, zhang (章) group, and zhuang (莊) group—posits them as alveolar fricatives, Alveolo-palatal fricatives, and lamino-postalveloar or apical postalveloar fricatives, respectively. However, while studying the third and fourth Deng as well as chongniu (重纽) in Yunjing (《韵镜》), the author proposes that the zhang group should be reconstructed as lamino-alveolar fricatives,transcibed as [sʲ], the jing group as a labialized lamino-alveolar fricatives[(sʲ)w], and the zhuang initials as palatalized lamino-alveolar fricatives [(sʲ)j],. Through an analysis of relevant sounds databases, it was found that these three sets of sibilant fricatives are either partially or fully preserved in most Min, Yue (Cantonese), and Hakka dialectal varieties across Fujian, Guangdong, Hainan, and Taiwan etc.. They also persist to varying degrees in dialects within Guangxi, as well as in Wu, Hui, and Jin dialects. Just as palatalized alveolo-palatal fricatives do , there exist among Chinese dialects 2 types of palatalized lamino-alveolar fricatives, both types attain upper vocal tract occlusion which serve as glottal medials. 切韵齿音精组章组莊组的拟音主流认为是舌尖前擦音[s]、舌面前擦音[ɕ]以及舌叶擦音[ʃ]或卷舌音[ʂ]及其塞擦音。作者在研究《韵镜》三等四等以及重纽过程中发现章组书母拟音为舌叶齿龈擦音,可标识为[sʲ],精组心母拟音为唇化舌叶齿龈擦音[(sʲ)w],莊组生母声纽拟音为颚化舌叶齿龈擦音[(sʲ)j]。经对相关语音库数据分析研究后发现,这3组有咝擦音或部分或完整地留存在福建广东海南台湾区域内的大多数语言点的闽方言粤方言和客家话中以及不同程度地留存在广西区域内的语言点和吴方言赣方言徽方言晋方言中。而颚化舌叶齿龈擦音[(sʲ)j]在共时音中也如颚化舌面前擦音[ɕj]一样也分为第1类和第2类,2类颚化擦音中均留存着上声道阻塞。因为历时音中出现了第2类的颚化舌叶齿龈擦音,因此切韵中的莊组和章组得以合并为三十六字母中的照组。 关键词:切韵齿音 舌叶齿龈擦音 唇化舌叶齿龈擦音 颚化舌叶齿龈擦音 上声道阻塞
