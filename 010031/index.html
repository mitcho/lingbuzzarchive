Split Antecedents Across Conjunctions
Yusuke Yagi
February 2026
 

This study proposes and defends dependency-building dynamic conjunction, which tracks discourse dependencies established within its conjuncts. The proposal is motivated by novel empirical data from English and Japanese, in which the antecedents of a pronoun are spread across conjuncts. The resolution of the anaphora requires keeping track of the discourse dependencies established in the conjuncts as well as internal and external dynamicity. the dependency-building is a characteristic of the universal quantifier. This study suggests that conjunction should (at least sometimes) model dependencies as the universal quantifier does, and that the conjunction and universal quantifier are closer to each other than conventionally thought in the literature on dynamic semantics.
