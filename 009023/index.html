A non-operator movement approach to Geis-ambiguities
Qiuhao Charles Yan
May 2025
 

The primary goal of this paper is to argue against the conventional operator movement approach to Geis-ambiguities. The motivation for favoring a non-operator movement account consists of two empirical findings. On the one hand, the wh-element when that introduces temporal adverbial clauses is better analyzed as a base-generated wh-complementizer rather than a mobile wh-operator. On the other hand, the ambiguous interpretations of adverbial when have some close correlation with different uses of specific bridge verbs (i.e., SAY verbs). Based on these obversations, this paper proposes an alternative approach that appeals to feature valuation and Agree instead of any kind of movement.
