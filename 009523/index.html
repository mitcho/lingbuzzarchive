Mereological Syntax: Phrase structure, cyclicity, and islands
David Adger
December 2025
 

[new version with cover!] The Open Access version of Mereological Syntax. Also available directly from MIT Press at the following URL (and do download a copy from there to highlight to MIT how cool Open Access is!) [https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/6062/Mereological-SyntaxPhrase-Structure-Cyclicity-and] The book argues for a reconceptualization of phrase structure so that, rather than combining two elements to make a new one (as Merge does), one element is combined with another becoming part of it. I show how this very simple but quite radical rethinking improves on bare phrase structure in numerous ways, and, with the addition of a very natural constraint on structure building, derives successive cyclicity as a theorem, and opens up the way to a new theoretical typology of islands and their exceptions.
