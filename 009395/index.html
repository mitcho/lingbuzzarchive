BAVARIAN COMPS – AS PLAIN AS SIMPLE
Hubert Haider
October 2025
 

This is a squib on doubly-filled and on 'inflected' Comps and an exercise in dealing with a tight spot. The wide variety of doubly-filled Comps in Bavarian dialects plus a grammatically multifunctional element confuses analysts. The grammatical resolution is simple, the complication is “decision overload”. [typo-corrected in Fn.11].
