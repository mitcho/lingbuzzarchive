A Brief Note on Dynamic Antisymmetry , Merge-based Theory , and its Implications to Early Child English Possessive {‘s} and the Setting of Word Order
Joseph Galasso
December 2024
 

Early child stage-1‘merge-based’ structures make licit only simpleton sister relations {x, y} which project prosaic lexical items at the expense of a fully functional/categorical stage showing inter alia Possessives/Genitives {‘s}. Furthermore, in addition to the classic benchmarks discussed in the literature regarding under/non—specification of INFL/Agreement, I feel a new extension can be made towards Moro’s idea—supported by my data—that DA (viz., the lack of recursive hierarchy as found in simpleton merge-operations), offers us a window into how the eventual setting of fixed word order in child syntax is connected to the onset of Possessive structures. I suppose this might constitute a new ‘twist’ in how the Minimalist Program can capture, as a universal operation, a language-specific (English) fixation of word-order to the projection of possessive {‘s}.
