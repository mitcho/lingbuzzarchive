The Syntax of Natural Language, 2nd edition
Michael Diercks, Beatrice Santorini, Tony Kroch
January 2025
 

An Open Access introductory textbook to the syntax of natural language from the perspective of generative syntactic theory. Suitable for undergraduate courses and introductory graduate courses. A revised edition of Santorini and Kroch (2007), previously published online. Comments, critiques, and copy-editing comments are welcome.
