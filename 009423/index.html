A structural ambiguity account for non-local allomorphy and *ABA violations using complex affixes
Daniar Kasenov
October 2025
 

Locality is considered a core condition on application of Vocabulary Insertion rules (Embick 2010; Bobaljik 2012). The evidence comes from adjacency requirements on contextual allomorphy and *ABA generalizations in various morphological domains. Both have been met with counterexamples (Moskal & Smith 2016; Ganenkov 2020; Dolatian & Guekguezian 2023; Ganenkov 2018; Middleton 2021; Davis 2021; Zompì 2023; Caha 2024b among others). The proposal of this paper rests on the idea of complex affix formation (Bobaljik 2012; Matushansky 2025): functional heads forming a constituent in exclusion of the terminal that undergoes allomorphy via base-generation. Based on a novel explicit formalization of a DM-style system with bottom-up Vocabulary Insertion and non-terminal Vocabulary Insertion rules, I provide novel analyses for a range of cases of non-local allomorphy and *ABA violations.
