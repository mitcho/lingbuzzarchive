The plus and minus of bonobo call combinations
Emmanuel Chemla, Thomas Brochhagen, Shane Steinert-Threlkeld
April 2025
 

Berthet et al. (2025) propose a computational method to find cases of non-trivial compositionality (when an utterance of a sequence "A B" should not just be interpreted as "A and B", as if elements A and B were interpreted one after the other for instance) in bonobo vocal communication. We show weaknesses of the criteria, and suggest a path for completing the program.
