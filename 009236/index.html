Agreement in the wrong place? Pospelov's hypothesis, predicatives and the homonymy of predicatives and adjectives in Russian
Anton Zimmerling
August 2025
 

I discuss the syntactic-semantic interface of Russian with focus on non-agreeing nominal predicatives. I check the claims previously advanced by Nicolai S. Pospelov and Lev V. Shcherba about the correlation between the emantics of predicatives and the absence of the subject-predicate agreement. The homonymy of non-agreeing predicatives and agreeing adjectival elements is a milestone of Russian grammar. The assignment of the predicative instrumental to adjectival elements lacking a non-sentential subject controller is triggered by raising of the sententional argument.
