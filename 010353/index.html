A REPLY TO TERENCE TAO’S OPEN LETTER “A SEVERE MISALIGNMENT OF AI IN MATHEMATICS” (Reflections relevant to linguistics, too)
Stela Manova
September 2026
 

I see the concern about AI and the future of scientific fields such as pure mathematics and linguistics somewhat differently. AI opens new perspectives much more than it closes disciplines—everything is a matter of a standpoint.
