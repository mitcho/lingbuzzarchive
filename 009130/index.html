TAG is not a Construction Grammar, it is a Transformational Grammar, or is it? — A comment on the nature of grammatical relations
Stefan Müller
October 2025
 

This paper discusses recent claims about Tree Adjoining Grammar (TAG) being suited as the formalization for phrasal Construction Grammar approaches. It is shown that inheritance as such is not a sufficient means to establish relations between linguistic objects and to capture generalizations. Of course, TAG has all the technical tools that are needed for expressing the generalizations, but if they are used appropriately, TAG is more similar to a Transformational Grammar than to a Construction Grammar. This discussion helps to show where the shortcomings of phrasal Construction Grammar proposals are and what should be done instead if one wants to assume a constructionist approach.
