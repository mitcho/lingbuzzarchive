A mirror-principle violation meets Nanosyntax: The case of Czech X-marking
Pavel Caha
March 2026
 

In Czech, the conditional (used in X-marking) is based on the past tense. The containment between the past and the conditional is not unique to Czech. The literature (including Von Fintel and Iatridou 2023, Majdić 2025) found that such a pattern is also attested in other languages. However, there is a difference between Czech and other languages: while Czech appears to have the order VERB-PAST COND (čet-l by), other languages manifest the order VERB-COND-PAST. This chapter proposes a nanosyntactic account of this (apparent) paradox.
