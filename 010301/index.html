“ERGATIVITY, properly aligned” – And the case of SHIPIBO
Hubert Haider
September 2026
 

Common sense is prone to error, especially when it comes to scientific matters. “Ergativity” is a concept fraught with bias. Not every language that appears to be ergative actually is one. SHIPIBO is a very good example. It is a DIFFERENTIALLY-marked-subject language, mistakenly considered to be ergative. This SQUIB is the answer to reviews of "Ergativity, properly aligned".
