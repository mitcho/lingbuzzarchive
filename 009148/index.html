Conditional wh-questions with VP Ellipsis
Satoru Ozaki
January 2026
 

I discuss a construction I call conditional wh-questions with VP Ellipsis, which are root object wh-questions that appear with an if-clause, in which there is VP Ellipsis. Two interpretations of the elided object are allowed: a wh-bound variable or a narrow-scope indefinite. Structural diagnostics reveal that the interpretation of the elided object is correlated with the height of the if-clause: the wh-bound reading obtains iff the if-clause is attached inside the wh-question. The indefinite reading of the elided object is interesting because there the elided object apparently mismatches with its antecedent, a (reconstructed) wh-phrase, in two ways: they differ in their syntactic forms (a non-wh indefinite vs. a wh-phrase) as well as scope (a narrow-scope indefinite vs. a wide-scope existential quantification that scopes over the question operator). Combining a PF-deletion theory of ellipsis with a semantic identity condition and independently motivated mechanisms for interpreting wh-questions and indefinites, I develop an account of the height-interpretation correlation of the elided object that also explains why the elided object with an indefinite reading is allowed the mismatch that it is: through the appropriate analytical lens, the apparent mismatch is not genuine.
