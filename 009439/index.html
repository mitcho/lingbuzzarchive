Subjunctive Mood in Tigrinya
Gioia Cacchioli
June 2025
 

The goal of this squib is to contribute to a better understanding of subjunctive mood by providing novel data on the understudied Ethio-semitic language Tigrinya (SOV, Eritrea and northern Ethiopia). I suggest that the clausal prefix kɨ-, that is selected by many different verb classes and is used to build several different constructions, is a subjunctive marker. I support this idea by presenting a varied amount of data that shows that kɨ- appears (for the most part) in contexts that trigger subjunctive mood in other languages.
