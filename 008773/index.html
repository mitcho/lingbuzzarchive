Null subjects in Old Germanic
Henrik Rosenkvist
September 2013
 

An unpublished chapter about null subjects in Old Germanic. I argue that the reason why 3p null subjects are frequent in OG (in contrast with 1p and 2p subjects) has to do with context identification/reference switch, and not with verb agreement.
