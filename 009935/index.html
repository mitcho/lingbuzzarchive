Standalone Sentence-final Particles: A Cross-linguistic Study of Japanese ne and Sichuan Mandarin ga
Zhiguo Xie
April 2026
 

This paper develops a phase-based analysis of so-called Standalone SFP constructions in Japanese and Sichuan Mandarin, arguing that they are derived via PF deletion of the complement of sentence-final particles within a restricted transfer domain. I propose a no-interphasal string deletion condition, according to which deletion must be confined within a single transfer domain; cross-phase deletion is therefore ruled out due to computational inefficiency at the SM interface. Under this approach, particles such as Japanese ne and Sichuan Mandarin ga are analyzed as heads of AddrP, a phase head, allowing their complements to be fully elided and yielding Standalone Response and Standalone Gap patterns. By contrast, SFPs such as Japanese yo and Sichuan Mandarin sae are analyzed as non-phasal Commit heads, whose complements span multiple phase domains, resulting in illicit deletion. The analysis is further extended to related PF ellipsis phenomena, including VP-ellipsis, sluicing, argument ellipsis, and CP-as-argument deletion, thereby providing a unified account of cross-construction ellipsis patterns.
