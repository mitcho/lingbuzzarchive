Concord is Insatiable Agree: Heterogeneous Case and Phi Patterns in Slavic and Finnic
Adam Przepiórkowski
April 2025
 

A general theory of nominal concord in terms of (insatiable) Agree is provided, together with its application to about the most quirky concord phenomena in Slavic and Finnic.
