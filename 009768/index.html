Generating language and decoding brains
Riny Huijbregts
September 2025
 

Can we read other people’s minds by seeing the neuroimaging data of their brains? Recent discussion claims success, but success is self-reinforced by design. More data and better statistics yield a better approximation to a mass of unanalyzed data. But approximation of language production is essentially different from explanation of language generation. Causal explanations of generative grammar may be confirmed by some statistical experiment; in contrast, a statistical result must still be causally explained before some theoretical understanding can be reached. Probability theory is incapable in principle of advancing scientific understanding beyond a measure of prediction based on statistical calculations that have no neurobiological reality in human brains.
