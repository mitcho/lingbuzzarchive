'Affect' in Binding and Coreference
Yoshihisa Kitagawa
May 1995
 

Binding and coreference are established when referentially underspecified pro-forms (including expletives) undergo Copy, Link or Move/Attract in the course of the derivation toward the C-I interface. This approach provides straightforward accounts for many thought-provoking phenomena discussed in the literature related to reference such as pronouns of laziness, VP ellipsis, reflexive dependency, Bach-Peters' Paradox, and expletives.
