A Forgotten Group of Middle Chinese Initials
Yanguang炎光 Guo郭
August 2026
 

There exist among various Sinitic dialects a group of Alveolo-palatal fricatives that are so close in terms of phonetic attributes to either [tʃ] group consonants or [tɕ] group consonants that they were not identified approriately and not transcribed with suitable IPA symbols during past field surveys. One of the reasons is so far there is no defined phonetic features for the forgotten sibilants. Middle Chinese rhyme books kept records of the sibilants, i.e. Group Zhuang initials (莊组声纽), however heretofore they are not yet deciphered with correctly designated phonetic features. This paper rediscovers and identifies phonetic features for this group of sibilants. In the process this group of sibilants are identified at the end of the initials with presence of upper vocal tract occlusion which serve as medials of glottal stop for characters under Group Zhuang initials with 2nd Deng in the Middle Chinese rhyme tables. Key words: Palatalized alveoro-palatal fricatives [tɕʲ] group consonants Group Zhuang initials Upper vocal tract occlusion 在国内一些方言里存在着这样一组舌面龈腭擦音,因为其在语音特征上非常接近于舌叶擦音[tʃ]组或舌面擦音[tɕ]组,所以在过往的语言点调查中没有被正确识别和用合适的国际音标来标音。原因可以追溯到至今国内语言学界尚未有对其语音特征进行过描述。中古音韵书记录了这一组擦音,即莊组声纽,但其至今尚未有正确的构拟音。 本文对这一组有咝擦音的语音特征进行了辨别和确认,在过程中发现此组声母含有上声道阻塞的特征,也因此推测出了《韵镜》的二等具有喉塞音作为介音的语音特征。 关键词:颚化的舌面龈腭擦音 [tɕʲ]组擦音 莊组声纽 上声道阻塞
