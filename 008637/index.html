Resumption as a novel DP/NP diagnostic
Imke Driemel, Abigail Bimpeh, Reginald Duah
December 2024
 

In this snippet, we provide an argument in favour of a DP/NP distinction correlating with the semantic type of definiteness in Akan. Based on the assumption that uniqueness based definites are NPs and and anaphoric definites are DPs, we investigate the consequence of such an approach in a language that displays productive resumption.
