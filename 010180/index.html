Dynamic Versus Static Analysis: Is the LSA 2027 Summer Institute Guiding Linguistics in the Wrong Direction?
Stela Manova
July 2026
 

Can linguistics continue to analyze language in the same way after the emergence of LLMs? This article argues that AI has fundamentally changed our understanding of language and language generation. It discusses the distinction between dynamic and static analysis, warns that a DYNAMIC approach yields DESCRIPTION, while a STATIC one yields GENERATION, and asks whether the themes of the LSA 2027 Summer Institute adequately reflect the progress made in NLP in recent years.
