Gradual Deletion, Null Allomorphy, and Variation in Hixkaryana Nasality Agreement
Mildred Hacker
October 2024
 

The Hixkaryana language shows mysterious reductions preceding nasal consonants, including optional debuccalization of obstruents and null allomorphy of prefixes. The two processes can be effectively analyzed as minimally distinct extensions of a common response to nasality disagreement. McCarthy’s Gradual Deletion framework is leveraged to explain the relationship between these extensions, and ultimately shows us that prefix deletion is best analyzed as part of the same fundamental process as debuccalization. Variation in when and where the process occurs feeds speculation on its evolutionary history, which in turn informs a working synchronic implementation based on the intersection of Gradual Deletion and Output-Output Faithfulness.
