Indexical Shift and its interactions with Perspective Sensitive Items (PSIs): Indexical PSIs in Turkish
Metehan Oğuz
March 2025
 

The link between indexical shift and perspective shift is not well-understood. In indexical shift, indexicals nominals (e.g. I, you, here, tomorrow) are interpreted relative to a reported context, rather than the actual context of utterance. In perspective shift, perspective sensitive items (PSIs) (e.g. tasty, local, foreigner), are interpreted from the viewpoint of an individual other than the speaker. This study examines how PSIs and indexical shift interact in Turkish attitude reports, and shows that PSIs do not exhibit a uniform behavior. In light of novel data, this study shows that some PSIs, termed Indexical PSIs (e.g. motion verb gel ‘to come’, evidential markers), show shift together patterns with indexical nominals, being targeted by indexical shift. In other words, Indexical PSIs must shift when they appear in an indexical shifting context, and cannot shift without indexical shift. Other types of PSIs, on the other hand, do not interact with the occurrence of indexical shift. The existence of Indexical PSIs supports context over-writing approaches to indexical shift over alternative accounts that try to reduce indexical shift into simple feature deletion/impoverishment.
