Grammar. Linguistics. Language (Responding to Discussants)
Anton Zimmerling
November 2025
 

This paper published in “Herald of the Russian Academy of Sciences”, vol. 95 (2025) is the authorized and slightly extended English version of the polemic note on the competence of grammar published in the journal “Baltic accent” in 2024. I advocate a dualistic approach to grammar versus lexicon. Grammar always operates on sets including singleton sets. Not all language objects can be treated as signs, therefore the view that linguistics is a proper part of semiotics is false.
