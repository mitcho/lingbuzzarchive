The Unaccusative-Unergative Distinction in Mandarin Resultative Constructions
Fulang Chen
September 2025
 

The Unaccusativity Hypothesis (UH) is supported by unaccusative-unergative distinctions in English resultatives, but is challenged by the apparent lack of such distinctions in Mandarin resultatives. This paper shows that, despite appearances to the contrary, Mandarin transitive resultatives do exhibit unaccusative–unergative distinctions, and proposes a non-uniform analysis of Mandarin resultatives with unaccusative abd unergative matrix verbs, in accordance with UH. The proposal not only sheds new light on the Uniformity of Theta-Assignment Hypothesis, but also advances a general theory of resultative argument structure.
