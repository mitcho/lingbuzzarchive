On the binary format of functional features
Johan Rooryck
October 2025
 

In this paper, I argue that the features of functional categories universally conform to a specific equipollent binary format that I will call the Contrary Format, to distinguish it from the more usual Contradictory Format that is usually and implicitly assumed in equipollent binary +/– features or unary features. The term ‘contrary’ adopts Horn’s (1989) distinction between contrary and contradictory negation. To borrow an image, while contradictory values oppose black and white, contrary values oppose white (or black) to every shade of gray. I argue that the Contrary Format functions as a necessary condition for the types of concepts that are expressed as functional categories, and that this perspective on the nature of functional features allows for the discovery of nontrivial relations across grammatical categories. The paper is largely programmatic in nature, and therefore paints in broad strokes rather than providing the fine-grained detail that might be customarily expected.
