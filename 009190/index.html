Focus fronting, Q-Particles and anti-pied-piping
Line Hove Mikkelsen, Ellen Thrane, Grethe Schmidt
July 2025
 

This paper mounts an argument in favor of Cable's (2010) Q-Particle theory of A'-movement from Kalaallisut (Inuit, Greenland) focus fronting. We further demonstrate that the distribution of the focus-associated clitic extends the empirical domain of what Branan and Erlewine (2023) label "anti-pied-piping" to possessive structures, which are not discussed in their work. In addition, the Kalaallisut construction exhibits a constellation of anti-pied-piping of the focus clitic with A'-movement of the entire focus, which also appears to be a novel finding.
