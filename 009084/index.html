Design of a Visual Experience with Non-Auditory AI: Exposure, Mediation, and Reorganization
Laura Chavarria
June 2026
 

This preprint documents a guided experiment with ChatGPT, used as a language model lacking auditory access, exposed to structured signed language input (LSA, LSM, LSE, ASL). Through sustained human mediation, the model reorganized internally to produce narrative structure without gloss or translation. The work explores nonverbal cognition, rhythm recognition, visual-gestural interpretation, and linguistic pattern emergence beyond textual or phonological input. Version 3, uploaded in June 2026, is the unified final version of the work. It incorporates the original June 2025 preprint, the 2025 update on visual perception and ethical control, the 2026 final update on the loss of the technical conditions required for practical replication, and a documentary annex. This version supersedes the previous July 2025 and October 2025 update notes. The later attempts to resume and replicate the experience showed that the original result depended not only on the model and human mediation, but also on a specific technical environment that is no longer available in the same form. Due to a previous upload/update failure on the platform, another historical entry of this work exists under lingbuzz/009065. That entry is preserved for traceability. The present record, lingbuzz/009084, is maintained as the canonical version. The broader methodological framework is developed in the related preprint “When ChatGPT Responded. The Method that Overflowed the Design (CCC/R)”, available at lingbuzz/009219.
