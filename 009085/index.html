A structured synthetic dataset of English and Italian verb alternations for testing lexical abstraction via functional lexicon in LLMs.
Giuseppe Samo
June 2025
 

In this paper, we develop a structured dataset the investigation of lexical abstraction via functional words in LLMs, concentrating on the change-of-state (COS) and object-drop (OD) verb alternations classes in English and Italian. We present the dataset creation process and report the results of baseline experiments.
