Highlighting this over that ‒ a cognitive and frequency explanation of transitive variation
Patrik Austin
March 2025
 

This study provides the null explanation of Greenberg's dominance (Universal #1, etc.), based on the principle of parsimony, overruling Generative Grammar and 'Greenbergian' typology (Dryer, Hawkins, etc.) - especially Branching Theory.
