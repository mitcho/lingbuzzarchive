Clitic placement and ellipsis
Andrés Saab
June 2025
 

The theme of this chapter is the interaction between clitic placement and ellipsis. Many case studies are reviewed with the aim of adjudicating among the two main theories of clitics, namely, the Syntax Only and the PF movement approaches. According to the first view, syntax is enough to account for the distributional peculiarities of special clitics. In contrast, according to the PF movement view (and other PF alternatives), syntax alone cannot explain those peculiarities and further morphological or phonological manipulations are also needed. Four case studies are discussed: (i) the distribution of verbal ellipsis and clitic placement in Romance, (ii) the correlation between clitic climbing and the ban of modal ellipsis in Spanish, (iii) the ban of particle reduplication under verbal ellipsis in Hungarian, and (iv) the behavior of clitic clusters in a variety of ellipsis environments in Serbo-Croatian. The entire set of facts requires a theory of clitic placement and clitic clustering in which both syntax and morphology play a crucial role in the derivational histories of clitics.
