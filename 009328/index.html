Dual Number in Kazym Khanty: Not-at-issue content that does not project
Fedor Golosov
December 2024
 

The Projection Principle (Beaver et al. 2017) states that semantic content projects iffit is not-at-issue. This paper presentsa counterexample to this claim: the dual number in Kazym Khanty featuringthe duality implication that is not-at-issue content that does not project.
