ZË-RO v0.2 Note: Morphological Masking & Recovery in Albanian200 (Controlled Ablation)
Sokol Gora
March 2026
 

We report a deterministic diagnostic from ZË-RO’s Albanian200 analysis harnesses, testing whether schwa-heavy prefix morphology (notably për-/përë-) can mask root-aligned carrier-primary vowels in a small anchored subset (N=28). In the Compound Stress v0.2 harness, root-anchored retention is 64.3% for position (9/14, p=0.013) and 100.0% for order (14/14, p=0.001); all position mismatches are për-prefixed cases where the surface carrier primary becomes Ë while the anchor root primary is A/E. A controlled “Gegë-sim” perturbation (Ë-drop heuristics; not a claim about real dialect IPA) recovers the masked position cases to 100.0% retention (14/14, p=0.001), while order remains 100.0% (14/14, p<0.001). This note is a mechanistic masking-and-recovery probe: it documents a reproducible interaction between prefix schwa and carrier-primary extraction, and makes no claim of semantic proof or dialect truth.
