The Influence of English and Urdu Loanwords in Hindi
Himani Jain
October 2025
 

This paper explores the influence of English and Urdu loanwords on the Hindi language, examining how these words have entered everyday speech and cultural expressions. It discusses the linguistic impact at phonological, morphological, syntactic, and semantic levels, as well as the role of these loanwords in translation and communication. The paper highlights the historical and social contexts that facilitated linguistic borrowing, especially cultural interactions and language contact. Through analysis of examples from media, literature, and daily conversation, this study illustrates how loanwords enrich Hindi vocabulary and reflect ongoing language evolution.
