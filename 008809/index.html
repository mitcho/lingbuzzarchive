A representational account of Czech palatalization
Edoardo Cavirani, Guido Vanden Wyngaerd
February 2025
 

We discuss three patterns of palatalization in Czech, each of which is associated with certain suffixes, but whose effects are visible on the final consonant of the morpheme to the left of the suffix. We provide an analysis of the internal structure of the Czech phoneme inventory in terms of element theory, and extend that to the palatalizers. We analyse the palatalizers as floating elements, a fact which accounts for their lateral (leftward) effect, as well as some peculiar behaviour in the context of labials and the lateral /l/. We propose a substance-free analysis for these effects in terms of the strict CV framework.
