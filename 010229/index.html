M-Mobile and other ‘superfluous’ forms of nasal onsets in Kartvelian
Thomas Wier
August 2026
 

Kartvelian languages are famous for their exuberant consonant onset clusters that violate sonority sequencing principles, notably nasal onsets. In reality, there is substantial variation amongst them to what extent they exhibit such onsets. This paper will propose that variation amongst nasal onsets in the family derives not only from retention from the protolanguage but from separate processes of nasal onset deletion in Megrelian, nasal onset epenthesis in Laz, and a process of morphophonological reanalysis called ‘M-mobile’ in Georgian.
