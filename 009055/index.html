A convergence of space and time: The case of as long as and until in Czech
Anne-Li Demonie
June 2025
 

This paper investigates temporal "as long as" and "until" clauses in Czech, focusing on the syncretism of the subordinating conjunction "dokud", which introduces both clause types. While previous accounts attribute the ambiguity of this marker to semantic interaction with negation and aspect (Dočekal 2012) or to differences in syntactic scope (Ürögdi 2013), I propose an alternative analysis. Drawing on the behaviour of the Russian, Polish and Slovak marker po, which is used as a conjunction in temporal clauses, but also as a preposition in the nominal domain — I argue that the ambiguity at the clausal level can be reanalysed as a well-known alternation between locative and directional readings of adpositions in the spatial domain, (e.g., He was sitting "on" the chair vs. He sat down "on" the chair). In this view, I show that it is possible to explain the syncretism - and by extension the ambiguity - by assuming a typical Nanosyntactic containment relationship (Caha et al. 2025, Starke 2009) between Path and Place projections (Pantcheva 2011).
