From Clefts to Definiteness in Bangla and Beyond
Shrayana Haldar
October 2025
 

I provide empirical support for the claim that the definiteness of a DP can be decomposed into an existential quantifier and a cleft operator attached to the restrictor of this quantifier. As empirical evidence, I provide novel data from Bangla, where exactly the kind of DPs that otherwise don't allow a definite reading suddenly allows a definite reading when a cleft morpheme is put on top of them. I follow Doron (2025), in deriving the standard uniqueness/maximality presupposition of definites from the trivalence resulting from this cleft operator inside the restrictor of an existential, projecting to the matrix level according to the Strong Kleene schema. Additional data is presented from English bearing on the separability of the cleft operator and the indefinite determiner in definites in general. Moreover, in addition, the account advocated here predicts weaker, conditional presuppositions for definites, which I argue to be detectable, when appropriate contexts are provided. I argue that the standard uniqueness/maximality presupposition fails to account for these cases. The standard uniqueness/maximality presupposition, I suggest, can be derived from the presupposition my account predicts via proviso-like strengthening.
