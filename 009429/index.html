From Clefts to Definiteness in Bangla and Beyond
Shrayana Haldar
May 2026
 

I provide empirical support for the claim that the definiteness of a DP can be decomposed into an existential quantifier and a cleft operator attached to the restrictor of this quantifier. As empirical evidence, I provide novel data from Bangla, where exactly the kind of DPs that otherwise do not allow a definite reading suddenly allows a definite reading when a cleft morpheme is put on top of them. I point out how this account is similar to and different from other decompositional accounts of definites and the consequences of this new account for the English data that motivated the other decompositional accounts.
