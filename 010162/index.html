Writing Systems as Civilizational Technology: Introducing AXIS, MRA, 300CC, and MRT — A Semantic-First Framework for Cross-Cultural Knowledge Mapping in the Digital Age
Eddie EddieSLChan
August 2026
 

This paper introduces the AXIS Integrated Research Framework — a four-component system comprising the Macro Radical Alphabet (MRA), the 300 Component Clusters (300CC), the Mixed Radical Themes (MRT), and the AXIS coordinate system itself — and argues for its positioning as a foundational architecture for knowledge organisation in the digital age. The central claim is civilizational: Chinese writing systems encode four distinct historical strata, each corresponding to a stage of human civilisational development and its characteristic cognitive demand. These strata are not merely historical curiosities — they are simultaneously present in every Chinese character and compound, and any adequate learning and knowledge system must operate across all four levels. Existing approaches, including traditional radical-based classification (the 214-category Kangxi system), phonemic alphabets, and modern character-learning applications, each address at most one or two strata and are therefore structurally incomplete for the digital age. The paper makes three interconnected arguments. First, MRA proposes 26 semantic categories as a replacement for traditional radical classification and as a principled challenge to phonemic alphabets for the purpose of cross-linguistic knowledge organisation. Unlike phonemic alphabets, which encode language-specific sound systems, MRA encodes universal semantic domains that are present in every human language — making it a candidate for world-language mapping. Second, MRT extends MRA from agricultural-era literacy into digital-age knowledge acquisition, providing 26 thematic clusters organised by academic, professional, and social domain. Third, 300CC provides the bridge mechanism: approximately 300 phonosemantic clusters that connect the archaeological semantic layer (MRA) to the contemporary thematic layer (MRT), enabling any learner to traverse from oracle bone origin to modern professional vocabulary within a single coherent framework.
