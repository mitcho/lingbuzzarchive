Semantics of Tokyo Japanese
Junichi Iida
July 2025
 

This paper describes Tokyo Japanese semantics with a newly developed formal system of natural language semantics. The contributions of the system include its unified system of formal notation of Japanese particles, demystifying the various meanings of the exhaustive and neutral ga, wa, shika, and dakeo and the single phonological-semantic feature "$," which connects information structure and prosody. The manuscript is part of a wider project called Kyouro, which attempts to build a formal model of the grammar of Japanese, including phonology, syntax, and semantics, and to apply the model to a novel romanization system for Japanese language teachers and students.
