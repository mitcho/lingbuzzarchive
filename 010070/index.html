Merge as Graph Extension
Sasson Margaliot
July 2026
 

This paper proposes a graph-theoretic interpretation of Merge inside the Workspace. The Workspace is represented as a directed graph, and syntactic objects are represented as Paths or subgraphs inside that graph. An application of Merge preserves the Paths already present and adds a new accessible Path. The new Path can then participate in later applications of Merge inside the same accumulated Workspace. The proposal is stated inside a seven-Layer architecture, with C-I and A-P as external systems and five internal Layers of the Language Faculty: Semantic Component (Σ), A-Syntax, A-bar Syntax, Distributed Morphology (DM), and Phonological Component (Φ). Each internal Layer applies an ordered grammar of Extension Rules through Expand, constructs a new output graph by Relinearization, and transfers that graph to the next Layer. The Layers are traversed in one order in *generation* and in the reverse order in *comprehension*, while the same general Expand procedure applies across the internal Layers.
