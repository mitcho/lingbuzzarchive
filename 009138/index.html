Vietnamese post-verbal được: aspectuality, modality, predication, and speaker commitment
Trần Phan, Barbara Meisterernst
December 2023
 

This study first argues that được indicates only dynamic readings in the capacity of a post-verbal modal auxiliary. We propose that if a post-verbal được is not interpreted dynamically, it does not serve as a modal auxiliary, but is either an aspectual marker of telicity, an evaluative predicate, or a pragmatic marker. The study then addresses the syntactic and interpretive properties of post-verbal được, adopting the view that được heads a VP-internal functional projection when encoding either Aktionsart or dynamic modal meaning. In sentences with a refutatory force, sentence-final được is analyzed as a pragmatic marker of public commitment (Krifka 2015; Wiltschko & Heim 2016), signaling the speaker’s disbelief together with a commitment to the truth of the opposite of a previous proposition. Remarks on previous characterizations of rightward được are included when relevant.
