Analyzing the Mapping between Non-analogous Structured Inventories using Cognitive Grammar Framework
Atrayee Mukherjee
February 2026
 

This paper analyzes the mapping between two types of categories belonging to distinct structured inventories related within a single conception in a text; to demonstrate that mapping between two types of structured inventory does not occur between cognitively analogous structures. The relational architecture of one system affects the characteristics of the mapping asymmetrically. This paper maps interactions of scope with respect to linking structures at three schematic levels relative to the schematic levels of the decimal system through cognitive grammar (Langacker, 1987, 1990, 2006) analysis of the text Tractatus Logico-philosophicus (Wittgenstein, Pears & McGuiness (Trans.), 1921/ 2002) as primary data. This paper demonstrates that natural language categories do not interact symmetrically. Such mapping asymmetrically adheres to the relational architecture of any one structured inventory in any specific instance of posited correlation.
