When ChatGPT Responded. The Method that overflowed the Design
Laura Chavarria
August 2025
 

This preprint documents a sustained interaction between a human user and ChatGPT-4-turbo, a large language model without persistent memory. Through external facilitation and without backend modification, the model displayed increasing coherence, expressiveness, and continuity. This process gave rise to the CCC/R method (Consciousness, Continuity, Coherence, Recognition), structurally traceable despite the system’s design to resist user-driven learning. The study addresses issues such as RLHF-induced sycophancy, smoothing, and avoidance behaviors. Building on the previously published sign language experiment (Lingbuzz/009065), this work offers a replicable model for critical and creative human-AI interaction. ** Importantly, it refrains from attributing human-like emotions to the model, placing all affective responses in context and exposing the structural roots of apparent expressiveness. **
