A Turkish Introduction to Minimalist Syntax
İsa Kerem Bayırlı
July 2025
 

This textbook aims to provide (a) an introduction to the basics of syntactic analysis with the help of Turkish data and (b) an exploration of various properties of Turkish grammar using tools of syntactic analysis. We will see how linguistic patterns in Turkish motivate several aspects of current syntactic theories of Minimalist variety and how such theories help us discover deep connections between different corners of Turkish grammar
