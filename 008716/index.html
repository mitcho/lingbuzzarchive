Inside the Borders. Old Scandinavian in Old Russia [Не пересекая границ: древнескандинавский язык в Древней Руси ]
Anton Zimmerling
December 2024
 

This short paper is aimed at analyzing the Old Russian – Old Scandinavian bilingualism in Old Russia of the X-XII centuries. I discuss two possible cases of syntactic borrowing from Old Scandinavian involving non-trivial uses of impersonal constructions. Clarification: The syntactic borrowing hypotheses raised in this paper are orthogonal for the discussion about the role of the Varangian element in the Old Russian society. Neither do I claim that alternative explanations exploring the idea that early Slavic and early Germanic languages exhibit here the same trends of evolution are completely excluded. My point is that for the mentioned phenomena, the borrowing hypotheses contribute to the better understanding of Old Russian grammar. The title of my paper - "Inside the Borders" echoes the name the conference where it was presented - "Crossing the Borders".
